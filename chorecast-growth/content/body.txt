@lesson 1.4 | Germs and washing hands | 🧼 | home
hook: Germs are too small to see. How do we stop them making us sick?
learn: Germs are tiny living things. Some of them can make us sick.
learn: Washing your hands with soap and water gets rid of germs. Rub for about 20 seconds, then rinse and dry.
learn: Wash before eating, after the toilet, and after touching animals.

@lesson 1.1 | A balanced plate | 🍽️ | kitchen
hook: What does a healthy dinner look like?
learn: A balanced plate is about half vegetables and fruit, a quarter grains like rice, pasta or bread, and a quarter protein like meat, fish, eggs, tofu or beans.
learn: Milk, yoghurt and cheese help build strong bones.
learn: A mix of foods gives your body energy and helps it grow and repair.

@lesson 1.2 | Drinks | 💧 | kitchen
hook: Sports drink, fizzy drink or water: which does your body need after sport?

learn: Energy drinks often have caffeine and aren't suitable for kids.

@lesson 1.4 | Teeth and skin | 🦷 | home

learn: Sugary food and drinks feed germs that make holes in teeth.
learn: Protect your skin from the sun: {nz:slip, slop, slap and wrap~au:slip, slop, slap, seek and slide}.

@lesson 1.1 | Training smart | 💪 | garden
hook: How do athletes get fitter without getting hurt?
learn: Fitness has parts: heart and lung fitness (running, cycling), strength (push-ups, squats) and flexibility (stretching).
learn: Warm up before exercise and cool down after. Build up slowly instead of doing too much at once.
learn: Rest days let muscles repair and get stronger.

check: mcq | Teens should be active for at least… | an hour a day* | an hour a month | 5 minutes a week

@lesson 1.2 | Teen sleep | 🌙 | home
hook: Why do teenagers often feel wide awake at night and sleepy in the morning?
learn: Teens need about 8 to 10 hours of sleep a night.
learn: In the teenage years, the body clock shifts later, so it's harder to feel sleepy early.
learn: Helpful habits: the same wake-up time every day, morning daylight, no caffeine after lunch, and screens off well before bed.
try: Morning light helps reset your body clock :: mcq | How much sleep do teens need? | About 8 to 10 hours* | About 5 hours | About 14 hours

check: tf | A teenager's body clock tends to shift later. | true
check: mcq | Which can make it harder to fall asleep? | Caffeine late in the day* | Morning sunlight | A cool room
check: mcq | Sleep helps you… | learn, grow and stay well* | only feel lazy | nothing much
home: Keep a sleep diary for a week. Are you getting 8 to 10 hours?
skill: teen-sleep

@lesson 1.3 | Sun and water safety | 🏖️ | island
hook: Why can you get sunburnt on a cloudy day?
learn: UV rays from the sun burn skin and can cause skin cancer later in life. They pass through cloud.
learn: Protect yourself: {nz:slip, slop, slap and wrap~au:slip, slop, slap, seek and slide}. Put sunscreen on again every two hours and after swimming.
learn: In the water, swim between the flags at patrolled beaches, never swim alone, and check conditions first. If you're caught in a rip, stay calm, float and raise your hand for help.

@lesson 1.4 | First aid basics | 🩹 | home
hook: Someone gets hurt. What are the first things you do?
learn: First, check for danger to yourself and others. Then get help: in an emergency call {nz:111~au:000}, or get a grown-up.
learn: For a minor burn, cool it under cool running water for 20 minutes. Don't use ice, butter or creams.
learn: For a bleeding cut, press firmly on it with a clean cloth.
