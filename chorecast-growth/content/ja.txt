@lesson 1.1 | こんにちは | 👋 | home
hook: Japanese has its own letters. Let’s meet a few, and say hello.
word: こんにちは | hello | 👋 | wave | konnichiwa
word: ゆき | Yuki (a girl’s name) | 👧 | wave to her | Yuki
word: けん | Ken (a boy’s name) | 👦 | wave to him | Ken
say: «こんにちは、ゆきさん！» | Hello, Yuki! | Konnichiwa, Yuki-san!
learn: These letters are hiragana. «こんにちは» is ko-n-ni-chi-wa. The last letter, «は», is said “wa” here.
try: trace | «こ» | ko
play: hear | Which one is «こんにちは»? | 👋* | 🌙 | 🍎
check: hear | What does «こんにちは» mean? | hello* | good night | thank you
check: mcq | Ken says «こんにちは、ゆきさん！» What is he doing? | Saying hello to Yuki* | Saying goodbye | Saying thank you
home: Say «こんにちは» to everyone at home today.
skill: ja-greet

@lesson 2.2 | さん、くん、ちゃん | 🙂 | stage
hook: In Japanese, you add a little word after someone’s name.
word: さん | (polite, after a name) | 🙂 | a small bow | san
word: くん | (after a boy’s name) | 👦🏻 | wave to a boy | kun
word: ちゃん | (after a little kid’s or a close friend’s name) | 👧🏻 | wave to a little kid | chan
