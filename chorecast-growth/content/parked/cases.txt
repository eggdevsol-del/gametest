@case graham | Benjamin Graham | invest | makers | 📘
meet: Benjamin Graham taught investing at university and wrote "The Intelligent Investor" in 1949. Warren Buffett was one of his students.
decide: Share prices crash and everyone is panicking. What would a careful investor do?
opt: Look at what the business is really worth => Graham said the market is like a moody partner, "Mr Market", who offers silly prices on bad days. Careful investors can use that.
opt: Sell everything because others are => That's following the crowd. Graham warned against letting Mr Market's mood become yours.
happened: Graham's ideas of a "margin of safety" (paying well below what something's worth) and ignoring market moods shaped generations of investors.
skill: Thinking for yourself, especially when everyone else is emotional.
turn: Next time a Chorecast fund drops, ask: has the business changed, or just the mood?
check: mcq | Who is "Mr Market"? | An imaginary moody partner who offers prices daily* | A real banker | A TV host
check: tf | A margin of safety means paying well below what something is worth. | true
