# Mandarin: course map

The whole path from first words to speaking on your own (around HSK 3.0 band 4), planned before the lessons are
written. Stages 1 to 4 follow the Youth Chinese Test levels (YCT 1 to 4, about HSK 1 and 2), stages 5 to 8 the HSK
3.0 bands 2 to 4. Each stage is 10 units of 8 lessons, plus a story and a challenge per unit.

**For the fluent speaker reviewing this:** simplified characters and Putonghua pronunciation, with pinyin shown above
the characters for 7 to 9-year-olds always and for everyone in stages 1 and 2. Tones are trained with "Which did you
hear?" pairs in several voices. Kids trace characters over a faint copy (without stroke order yet). Please check the
characters, the pinyin and tone marks (including 一 and 不 tone changes), and that everything sounds natural for
children. Politics and religion stay out.

## Stage 1 (YCT 1): 你好！ (written: `parked/zh.txt`)

| Unit | Kids can… | Words and phrases | Patterns |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 你好！ | greet, thank and say goodbye | 你好, 早上好, 晚安, 再见, 明天见, 谢谢, 不客气, 对不起, 没关系, 请, 是, 不是, 好的, 老师好, 小明, 丽丽 | tones on every syllable |
| 2 你叫什么名字？ | say who they are and introduce others | 我, 你, 叫, 什么, 名字, 他, 她, 是, 谁, 朋友, 人, 新西兰, 澳大利亚, 中国, 男孩, 女孩, 学校, 老师, 好朋友 | 我叫…; 他是…; 我是新西兰人 |
| 3 你好吗？ | say how they feel; hear the four tones | 妈, 马, 吗, 好, 很, 高兴, 难过, 累, 生气, 害怕, 无聊, 饿, 渴, 冷, 热, 生病, 疼, 有点儿, 没事, 太棒了, 漂亮, 做得好 | 吗 questions; 我很…; 我饿了 |
| 4 数字 | count, say their age, talk about money | 一 to 十, 十一 to 二十, 三十, 一百, 几, 岁, 个, 两, 有, 钱, 块 | 我八岁; 我有三个朋友; 五块钱 |
| 5 颜色 | name colours and describe their island | 红色, 黄色, 绿色, 蓝色, 白色, 黑色, 橙色, 粉色, 棕色, 紫色, 灰色, 颜色, 房子, 门, 屋顶, 窗户, 篱笆, 花, 太阳, 云, 天空, 彩虹 | the colour comes first: 红房子; 门是绿色的 |

| 8 吃的 | ask for food and buy at the farm stand | 吃, 喝, 水, 牛奶, 苹果, 香蕉, 橙子, 面包, 鸡蛋, 米饭, 土豆, 胡萝卜, 要, 给, 买, 卖, 商店, 多少钱, 好吃, 饱 | 我要…; 请给我…; 多少钱？ |

## Stage 2 (YCT 2)

1. Pinyin in full: every initial and final, and the tone changes of 一 and 不.
2. Likes: 我喜欢…, 你喜欢…吗？
3. Home: rooms and things in them; 有 for "there is".
4. My day: 起床, 吃早饭, 上学; telling the time (几点？).
5. Clothes and the weather.
6. The body; 我头疼.
7. Sport and games; 我会…
8. School subjects.
9. Describing people: 高, 矮, 可爱.
10. First 100 characters, read and traced.

## Stage 3 (YCT 3)

1. Past with 了 and experience with 过.
2. Plans with 要 and 想.
3. Comparing with 比.
4. Shopping, prices and change.
5. Directions in town.
6. Food and cooking.
7. Asking for and giving help.
8. Writing a short message.
9. A short story read aloud.
10. Characters: radicals, and how they hint at meaning.

## Stage 4 (YCT 4, about HSK 2)

1. Duration and frequency.
2. Measure words in depth.
3. Health and feelings.
4. Nature, animals and caring for the planet.
5. Travel and transport.
6. Money and work: jobs, pay and saving (built on Chorecast's jars).
7. Opinions and reasons: 因为…所以…
8. Festivals as culture (Spring Festival, Mid-Autumn), not religion.
9. Reading short texts for young readers.
10. About 300 characters read.

## Stages 5 to 8 (HSK 3.0 bands 2 to 4)

- **Stage 5:** result and direction complements; the 把 sentence; telling events in order.
- **Stage 6:** the passive 被; persuading; advice; longer conversations.
- **Stage 7:** linking words (虽然…但是…, 如果…就…); summarising; idioms in everyday speech.
- **Stage 8:** discussion and debate with respect; adapting language for different audiences; a final talk on a topic they choose. About 1,200 characters read and 3,000 words known.
