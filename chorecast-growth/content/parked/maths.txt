@lesson 1.1 | Adding money | ➕ | stall
hook: An apple is 50c and a banana is 40c. Do you have enough with $1?
learn: To add money, add the cents, then the dollars.
learn: 100 cents makes $1.
try: 50c + 40c = 90c :: gap | 30c + 50c = _c | 80
play: gen | add | 4
check: gap | 60c + 40c = _c | 100
check: mcq | 100 cents is the same as… | $1* | $10 | 10c
check: tf | 50c + 50c = $1 | true
home: Add up the prices of two things on a shopping list.
