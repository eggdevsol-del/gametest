@lesson 3.1 | 四个声调 | 🎵 | garden
hook: Mandarin has four tones. The same sound in a different tone is a different word!
word: 妈 | mum (high and flat: mā) | 👩 | hold your hand flat and high | mā
word: 马 | horse (dips down and up: mǎ) | 🐴 | dip your hand down and up | mǎ
word: 吗 | (a question word, light: ma) | ❓ | a tiny tap | ma
rule: The four tones go high and flat (mā), up (má), down and up (mǎ) and sharply down (mà). A light, short syllable has no mark (ma).
try: trace | «马» | horse
play: pair | Which did you hear? | «妈»* | «马»
check: pair | Which did you hear? | «马»* | «妈»
check: hear | What does «马» mean? | horse* | mum | a question word
home: Say «妈» and «马» to someone. Can they hear the difference?
skill: zh-tones

@lesson 3.2 | 你好吗？ | 🙂 | garden
hook: How are you?
word: 好 | good, well | 👍 | thumbs up | hǎo
word: 很 | very | ⭐ | arms out wide | hěn
say: «你好吗？» | How are you? | Nǐ hǎo ma?
say: «我很好。» | I’m good. | Wǒ hěn hǎo.
rule: «很» means very, but Chinese puts it before words like «好» anyway, so «我很好» is just “I’m good”.
rule: «吗» at the end turns a sentence into a yes-or-no question: «你好» (hello), «你好吗？» (are you well?).
play: tiles | Build it: I’m good. | 我 很 好
check: mcq | Your friend asks «你好吗？» You answer… | «我很好。»* | «再见。» | «不客气。»
check: hear | What does «你好吗» mean? | How are you?* | Hello! | Who are you?
home: Ask someone at home «你好吗？»
skill: zh-feel
