learn: Future value of regular deposits = payment × ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1) ÷ r, with r and n in the same periods.

@lesson 2.5 | How long to double? | ⌛ | bank
hook: How many years until $5,000 becomes $10,000 at 7%? The rule of 72 says about 10. Can you get it exactly?
learn: Solve (1 + r)ⁿ = 2 with logs: n = log 2 ÷ log(1 + r).
learn: At 7%: 0.301 ÷ 0.0294 ≈ 10.2 years.
learn: The same idea finds any target: n = log(target ÷ start) ÷ log(1 + r).

@lesson 2.6 | When compounding works against you | 📉 | bank
hook: Compounding builds wealth. What happens when it runs the other way?
learn: Debt compounds. Unpaid credit card interest joins the balance, and then that gets charged interest too.
learn: Inflation compounds. At 3% a year, prices rise by about a third in 10 years.
learn: Fees compound. A 1% yearly fee can shrink a 40-year balance by around 30%.
