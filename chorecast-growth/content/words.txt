@lesson 1.1 | Capital letters and full stops | ✍️ | stage

@lesson 1.2 | Reasons and evidence | 🧾 | stage

learn: Strong evidence is specific and comes from a source the reader can trust.

check: mcq | Good evidence is… | specific and trustworthy* | vague and exciting | as long as possible
