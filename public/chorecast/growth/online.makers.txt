@lesson 1.1 | Phishing | 🎣 | online
hook: An email from "Netflix" says your payment failed. The link goes to netf1ix-billing.com. Spot it?
learn: Phishing tricks you into giving passwords or card details through fake messages and websites.
learn: Check the sender, hover over links, and log in through the real app instead.
play: scam | Real or phishing? | Login alert from your bank app, inside the app=real; Email from support@netf1ix-billing.com asking you to update your card=scam; Text: Your parcel is held. Pay $2.99 at this link=scam; Your school's portal, which you opened yourself=real; DM from a "brand" saying you've won, click to claim=scam | Look-alike addresses, small payments and prize links are classic phishing.
check: mcq | What's suspicious about "netf1ix-billing.com"? | The 1 instead of an l, and an odd domain* | Nothing | It's too short
check: tf | Logging in through the real app is safer than clicking email links. | true
check: mcq | You clicked a phishing link and typed your password. Now… | Change your password and turn on 2FA* | Wait and see | Tell nobody
home: Forward a suspicious message to {nz:Netsafe or the bank~au:Scamwatch or your bank}, with a grown-up.

@lesson 1.4 | Fake online stores | 🛒 | online
hook: Brand-name sneakers, 80% off, on a website you've never heard of. Bargain?
learn: Fake stores use huge discounts, new domains, no contact details and pressure to pay by bank transfer.
learn: Check reviews from other sites, and pay with methods that offer protection.
play: scam | Real store or fake? | Big-name store you know, normal prices, clear returns policy=real; Brand-name shoes 85% off, bank transfer only=scam; Store with a physical address, phone number and reviews on other sites=real; Site created last week with countdown timers on every item=scam | Huge discounts, transfer-only payments and brand-new sites are red flags.
check: mcq | Which payment usually offers more protection? | A credit card or trusted payment service* | Bank transfer to a stranger | Gift cards
check: tf | A deal that seems too good to be true often is. | true
check: mcq | Where should you check reviews? | On other trusted sites* | Only on the store's own page | Nowhere
home: Check a store you use for contact details and a returns policy.
