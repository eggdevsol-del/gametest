@lesson 1.1 | What is good work? | ⭐ | workshop
hook: Two kids tidy their rooms. One is done in 2 minutes. One takes 10. Who did better?
learn: Good work is done properly, not just quickly.
learn: Check your work before you say "done".
play: tap | Tap the jobs that are NOT finished properly | 🛏️:yes:The bed still has lumps; 🧸:no; 🧦:yes:A sock is on the floor; 📚:no; 🍽️:yes:A dirty plate is still there; 🪴:no; 👟:no; 🖍️:yes:Crayons left out
check: mcq | What makes work good? | It's done properly* | It's done fast | Nobody saw it
check: tf | You should check your work before saying done. | true
check: mcq | Your bed has a big lump. Is it finished? | Not yet. Smooth it out* | Yes, close enough | Hide it with a pillow
home: Do one chore today and check it twice before you say done.
skill: quality

@lesson 1.2 | Doing it in order | 🔢 | home
hook: Can you make a sandwich if you start with the top slice of bread?
learn: Many jobs work best in the right order.
learn: Planning the steps first saves time.
play: order | Put the sandwich steps in order | Wash your hands; Get two slices of bread; Spread the butter; Add the filling; Put the top slice on
check: mcq | What should you do first in most kitchen jobs? | Wash your hands* | Lick the spoon | Turn on the TV
check: tf | Planning the steps first can make jobs faster. | true
check: mcq | Why does order matter? | Some steps need others done first* | It doesn't | Because it's a rule for fun
home: Before your next chore, say the steps out loud first.
skill: planning

@lesson 1.3 | Community helpers | 🚒 | townhall
hook: Who comes when there's a fire?
learn: Lots of people do jobs that help everyone.
learn: Firefighters, nurses, teachers, rubbish collectors and bus drivers keep things running.
play: match | Match each helper to their job | Firefighter=Puts out fires; Nurse=Cares for sick people; Teacher=Helps you learn; Rubbish collector=Takes away rubbish; Bus driver=Gets people where they need to go
check: mcq | Who helps when you're sick? | A nurse or doctor* | A pilot | A baker
check: tf | Rubbish collectors do an important job. | true
check: mcq | Who drives people to school or work? | A bus driver* | A firefighter | A vet
home: Say thank you to a community helper this week.
skill: careers

@lesson 1.4 | Sticking with it | 🧗 | garden
hook: A puzzle is hard. Do you give up or keep going?
learn: Hard things get easier with practice.
learn: Taking a break and trying again is not giving up.
play: path | You're building a big Lego set and a piece won't fit. | Take a breath and check the instructions => good: You found the mistake and fixed it! | Throw the Lego => bad: Ouch. Now it's broken and you're still stuck. | Ask a grown-up to help => good: Asking for help is smart, and you learned how to fix it.
check: mcq | A job is tricky. What's a good move? | Take a break, then try again* | Give up forever | Get cross
check: tf | Asking for help is a smart thing to do. | true
check: mcq | What makes hard things easier? | Practice* | Wishing | Hiding
home: Pick something tricky and practise it for 5 minutes.
skill: grit

@lesson 1.5 | The right tool | 🧰 | workshop
hook: Could you sweep the floor with a fork?
learn: Every job has a tool that makes it easier.
learn: Using tools safely matters. Ask a grown-up about sharp or hot things.
play: match | Match each job to its tool | Sweeping=Broom; Watering plants=Watering can; Cutting paper=Scissors; Washing dishes=Sponge; Raking leaves=Rake
check: mcq | Best tool for watering plants? | A watering can* | A spoon | A sock
check: tf | You should ask a grown-up before using sharp tools. | true
check: mcq | Why use the right tool? | It makes the job easier and safer* | It looks cool | It doesn't matter
home: Help a grown-up with a job and name the tools you used.
skill: tools

@lesson 1.6 | Being a good teammate | 🤝 | home
hook: Your family is cleaning up after dinner. What's your job?
learn: In a team, everyone does a part.
learn: Good teammates help without being asked.
play: tf | Good teammates only do their own job and nothing else. | false | Good teammates help others too.
check: mcq | What does a good teammate do? | Helps others* | Does nothing | Bosses everyone
check: tf | Helping without being asked is a good thing. | true
check: mcq | After dinner, a good teammate might… | Clear their plate and help wipe the table* | Leave straight away | Hide
home: Help someone in your family without being asked today.
skill: teamwork
