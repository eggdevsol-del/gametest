@lesson 2.2 | Conflicts of interest | ⚖️ | townhall
hook: A board member's company wins a big contract from the charity they help run. Problem?
learn: A conflict of interest is when someone's personal interests could sway their decisions.
learn: Conflicts happen. Good governance means declaring them and stepping out of the decision.
learn: Hidden conflicts are where trouble starts.
play: path | You're on a youth club committee. Your aunt's bakery wants the club's catering contract. | Declare the link and step out of the vote => good: That's how conflicts are handled. The committee can decide fairly. | Vote for your aunt without saying anything => bad: That's a hidden conflict. Even if she's the best, trust is damaged. | Quietly vote against her to look fair => ok: Well meant, but the link is still swaying your vote. Declare it and step out.
check: mcq | What's a conflict of interest? | When personal interests could sway your decisions* | An argument at a meeting | A competition
check: tf | Having a conflict of interest is always wrongdoing. | false | Conflicts are normal. Hiding them is the problem.
check: mcq | The right way to handle a conflict? | Declare it and step out of the decision* | Keep it quiet | Vote twice
home: Find the conflicts of interest rule in a charity's, club's or council's rules.
skill: governance

@lesson 2.3 | Reading charity accounts | 📊 | bank

check: tf | The lowest admin spending always means the best charity. | false | Good systems and staff cost money and help charities do good work.
check: mcq | Which is a source of charity income? | Grants* | Programme spending | Admin costs

@lesson 2.4 | Board members' duties | 🏛️ | townhall
hook: Being on a board sounds like going to meetings. What are you actually responsible for?

learn: They must take reasonable care, understand the finances and ask hard questions.

check: tf | Board members should ask hard questions about the finances. | true
check: mcq | {nz:Which law sets out NZ company directors' duties?~au:Which law sets out Australian company directors' duties?} | {nz:The Companies Act 1993~au:The Corporations Act 2001}* | A sports club newsletter | The school timetable

@lesson 2.5 | Whistleblowing | 📣 | townhall
hook: You work at a charity and see donations paying for a manager's holidays. What can you do?
learn: Whistleblowing means reporting serious wrongdoing inside an organisation.
learn: {nz:The Protected Disclosures (Protection of Whistleblowers) Act 2022 protects people who report serious wrongdoing in the right way.~au:Australian law protects many whistleblowers who report misconduct in the right way, for example under the Corporations Act.}
learn: Write down what you saw. Report it through the proper channel, inside the organisation or to the right authority.
play: path | You see donation money paying for a manager's personal holidays. | Note the facts and report through the proper channel => good: That's how whistleblower protections are meant to work. | Post it all on social media straight away => bad: You could lose legal protection, and the facts might get twisted. | Say nothing because it's not your job => bad: The wrongdoing carries on, and donors keep being misled.
check: mcq | What is whistleblowing? | Reporting serious wrongdoing in an organisation* | Refereeing a game | Gossiping about a workmate
check: tf | The law can protect whistleblowers who report in the right way. | true
check: mcq | A good first step if you see wrongdoing? | Write down the facts* | Delete the evidence | Confront them in public
home: Find out who you could report serious wrongdoing to at a workplace or club you know.
skill: governance

@lesson 2.6 | Red flags | 🚩 | museum
hook: What warning signs come before an organisation's money goes missing?

check: tf | Good organisations welcome questions from their board and staff. | true
check: mcq | Why is "two people approve each payment" a good rule? | No one person can misuse the money alone* | It's twice as fast | It's just tradition
