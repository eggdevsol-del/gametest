@lesson 2.2 | Conflicts of interest | ⚖️ | townhall
hook: A board member's company wins a big contract from the charity they help run. Problem?
learn: A conflict of interest is when someone's personal interests could sway their decisions.
learn: Conflicts happen. Good governance means declaring them and stepping out of the decision.
learn: Hidden conflicts are where trouble starts.
play: path | You're on a youth club committee. Your aunt's bakery wants the club's catering contract. | Declare the link and step out of the vote => good: That's how conflicts are handled. The committee can decide fairly. | Vote for your aunt without saying anything => bad: That's a hidden conflict. Even if she's the best, trust is damaged. | Quietly vote against her to look fair => ok: Well meant, but the link is still swaying your vote. Declare it and step out.
check: mcq | What's a conflict of interest? | When personal interests could sway your decisions* | An argument at a meeting | A competition
check: tf | Having a conflict of interest is always wrongdoing. | false | Conflicts are normal. Hiding them is the problem.
check: mcq | The right way to handle a conflict? | Declare it and step out of the decision* | Keep it quiet | Vote twice
home: Find the conflicts of interest rule in a charity's, club's or council's rules.
