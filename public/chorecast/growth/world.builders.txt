@lesson 1.1 | Two kinds of tax | 🧾 | townhall
hook: You buy a $10 book. Do you pay tax?
learn: Income tax comes out of people's pay.
learn: GST is added to most things you buy: {nz:15% in New Zealand~au:10% in Australia}.
learn: Even kids pay GST when they buy things.
try: {nz:A $20 item before GST costs $23 with 15% GST~au:A $20 item before GST costs $22 with 10% GST} :: gap | {nz:$40 before GST. With 15% GST it's $_~au:$40 before GST. With 10% GST it's $_} | {nz:46~au:44}
play: gen | gst | 3
check: mcq | Which tax is added when you buy things? | GST* | Income tax | Pocket money tax
check: tf | Kids pay GST when they buy things. | true
check: mcq | Income tax comes from… | People's pay* | Library books | Rain
home: Find "GST" on a receipt.
skill: tax

@lesson 1.4 | Consumer rights | 🧾 | stall
hook: Your new headphones broke after two days. Can you get your money back?
learn: Products must be of acceptable quality, fit for purpose and as described.
learn: If they fail, you're usually entitled to a repair, replacement or refund. Keep your receipt.
play: chat | Returning broken headphones | them: Hi, how can I help? | me: I bought these headphones on Saturday and the left side stopped working. Here's my receipt*/These are rubbish, give me money/Never mind | them: Sorry about that! Would you like a replacement or a refund? | me: A replacement would be great, thanks*/I want both/Whatever
check: mcq | Your new product breaks quickly. You're usually entitled to… | A repair, replacement or refund* | Nothing | A free car
check: tf | Keeping your receipt helps with returns. | true
check: mcq | A good way to ask for a refund? | Calmly, with the receipt* | Shouting | Not asking
home: Find the returns policy of a shop you like.
skill: consumer

@lesson 1.5 | Supply and demand | ⚖️ | stall
hook: Why do strawberries cost more in winter?
learn: When lots of people want something and there isn't much, prices go up.
learn: When there's plenty and fewer people want it, prices go down.
play: match | Match the situation to the price change | Strawberries in winter=Price goes up; Strawberries in summer=Price goes down; A toy everyone wants at Christmas=Price goes up; Umbrellas after the rain stops=Price goes down
check: mcq | Little supply plus lots of demand means prices usually… | Go up* | Go down | Stay the same
check: tf | Seasons can change food prices. | true
check: mcq | Why are summer strawberries cheaper? | There are lots of them* | They taste worse | Shops are kind
home: Compare a fruit price in two different seasons.
skill: economy

@lesson 1.6 | Rights and responsibilities | 🤲 | townhall

check: mcq | Which is a responsibility? | Respecting others* | Free lunch every day | Nothing
home: Name one right and one responsibility you have at home.
