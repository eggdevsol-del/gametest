@lesson 1.5 | Supply and demand | ⚖️ | stall
hook: Why do strawberries cost more in winter?
learn: When lots of people want something and there isn't much, prices go up.
learn: When there's plenty and fewer people want it, prices go down.
play: match | Match the situation to the price change | Strawberries in winter=Price goes up; Strawberries in summer=Price goes down; A toy everyone wants at Christmas=Price goes up; Umbrellas after the rain stops=Price goes down
check: mcq | Little supply plus lots of demand means prices usually… | Go up* | Go down | Stay the same
check: tf | Seasons can change food prices. | true
check: mcq | Why are summer strawberries cheaper? | There are lots of them* | They taste worse | Shops are kind
home: Compare a fruit price in two different seasons.

@lesson 2.6 | Council budget choices | ⚖️ | townhall
