@lesson 1.1 | Adding money | ➕ | stall
hook: An apple is 50c and a banana is 40c. Do you have enough with $1?
learn: To add money, add the cents, then the dollars.
learn: 100 cents makes $1.
try: 50c + 40c = 90c :: gap | 30c + 50c = _c | 80
play: gen | add | 4
check: gap | 60c + 40c = _c | 100
check: mcq | 100 cents is the same as… | $1* | $10 | 10c
check: tf | 50c + 50c = $1 | true
home: Add up the prices of two things on a shopping list.
skill: adding

@lesson 1.2 | Giving change | 💵 | stall
hook: You pay $2 for a $1.50 ice block. How much comes back?
learn: Change is what you get back when you pay too much.
learn: Count up from the price to what you paid.
try: $1.50 up to $2 is 50c :: gap | Something costs $3. You pay $5. Change = $_ | 2
play: gen | change | 3
check: gap | A 70c lolly, paid with $1. Change = _c | 30
check: tf | Change is money you get back. | true
check: mcq | Price $4, you pay $5. Change? | $1* | $5 | $9
home: Play shop at home and give the right change.
skill: change

@lesson 1.3 | Telling the time | 🕒 | home
hook: Swimming starts at 4 o'clock. It's 3:30. Do you have time to eat?
learn: The short hand shows the hour. The long hand shows the minutes.
learn: Half past means 30 minutes after the hour.
play: match | Match the time words to the clock | 3 o'clock=3:00; Half past 3=3:30; Quarter past 3=3:15; Quarter to 4=3:45
check: mcq | Half past 7 is… | 7:30* | 7:00 | 7:15
check: tf | There are 60 minutes in an hour. | true
check: gap | It's 3:30. Swimming is at 4:00. That's _ minutes away. | 30
home: Tell a grown-up the time three times today.
skill: time

@lesson 1.4 | Halves and quarters | 🍕 | kitchen
hook: Two kids, one pizza. How do you share fairly?
learn: A half is one of 2 equal parts. A quarter is one of 4.
learn: Half of 8 is 4. A quarter of 8 is 2.
play: gen | percent | 3
check: gap | Half of 10 = _ | 5
check: gap | A quarter of 12 = _ | 3
check: mcq | 4 kids share a pizza equally. Each gets… | A quarter* | A half | The whole pizza
home: Cut a sandwich into quarters.
skill: fractions

@lesson 1.5 | Good guesses | 🤔 | home
hook: How many jellybeans are in the jar? Don't count. Guess!
learn: Estimating is making a smart guess.
learn: Use what you know. A handful is about 20.
play: slider | About how many pencils fit in a pencil case? | 0 | 100 | 20 | pencils | 10 | 1
check: tf | An estimate has to be exactly right. | false
check: mcq | About how long is a pencil? | 15 cm* | 15 metres | 1 cm
check: slider | About how many kids are in a class? | 0 | 100 | 25 | kids | 8 | 1
home: Guess how many steps from your bed to the door, then count.
skill: estimating

@lesson 1.6 | Catch the sale | ⏱️ | stall
hook: A price tag zooms past. Tap when it's on SALE!
learn: Sales make things cheaper for a short time.
learn: Saving money on things you already need is smart. Buying things you don't need because they're cheap isn't.
play: timing | Tap when the marker reaches the sale | Catch it! | SALE
check: tf | A sale is smart if you buy things you don't need. | false
check: mcq | A $10 toy is half price. It now costs… | $5* | $10 | $20
check: mcq | When is a sale a good deal? | When you needed it anyway* | Always | Never
home: Find a sale sign at the shop and work out the new price.
skill: sales

@lesson 2.1 | O'clock and half past | 🕐 | home
hook: The long hand points straight up. The short hand points at 3. What time is it?
learn: A clock has a short hand for hours and a long hand for minutes.
learn: When the long hand points to 12, it's something o'clock.
learn: When the long hand points to 6, it's half past.
play: match | Match each clock to its time | Short hand on 3, long hand on 12=3 o'clock; Short hand on 7, long hand on 12=7 o'clock; Short hand between 4 and 5, long hand on 6=Half past 4; Short hand between 9 and 10, long hand on 6=Half past 9
check: mcq | The long hand is on 12 and the short hand is on 8. It's… | 8 o'clock* | Half past 8 | 12 o'clock
check: tf | When the long hand points to 6, it's half past. | true
check: mcq | Which hand shows the hours? | The short hand* | The long hand | Neither
home: Find a clock at home. Read it at o'clock and at half past.
skill: time

@lesson 2.2 | Quarter past and quarter to | 🕒 | home
hook: Half an hour is half the clock. What is a quarter?
learn: Quarter past means 15 minutes after the hour. The long hand points to 3.
learn: Quarter to means 15 minutes before the next hour. The long hand points to 9.
learn: On a digital clock, quarter past 2 is 2:15. Quarter to 3 is 2:45.
play: match | Match the words to the digital time | Quarter past 2=2:15; Half past 2=2:30; Quarter to 3=2:45; 3 o'clock=3:00
check: mcq | Quarter past 5 on a digital clock is… | 5:15* | 5:45 | 5:25
check: gap | Quarter to 8 is 7:_ | 45
check: tf | Quarter to 4 is 15 minutes before 4 o'clock. | true
home: Say "quarter past" or "quarter to" each time you spot one on a clock today.
skill: time

@lesson 2.3 | Minutes and hours | ⏳ | home
hook: How many minutes are in one hour?
learn: There are 60 minutes in an hour.
learn: Half an hour is 30 minutes. A quarter of an hour is 15 minutes.
learn: There are 24 hours in a whole day.
try: 30 minutes + 30 minutes = 60 minutes, which is 1 hour :: gap | 15 + 15 + 15 + 15 = _ minutes | 60
play: sort | Less than an hour, More than an hour | Brushing teeth=Less than an hour; A night's sleep=More than an hour; Eating a snack=Less than an hour; A school day=More than an hour; Tying shoes=Less than an hour; A long car trip=More than an hour | How long does it take?
check: gap | Half an hour is _ minutes | 30
check: mcq | How many hours are in a day? | 24* | 12 | 60
check: tf | Brushing your teeth takes more than an hour. | false | It takes about 2 minutes.
home: Time how long it takes you to get dressed in the morning.
skill: time

@lesson 2.4 | Morning or afternoon? | 🌅 | home
hook: 7 o'clock can be breakfast time or dinner time. How do we tell?
learn: Am means the morning, from midnight to midday.
learn: Pm means the afternoon and night, from midday to midnight.
learn: Breakfast is around 7 am. Dinner is around 6 pm.
play: order | Put Saturday in order | Wake up at 7 am; Breakfast at 7:30 am; Sport at 10 am; Lunch at 12:30 pm; Dinner at 6 pm; Bed at 7:30 pm
check: mcq | You eat breakfast at about… | 7 am* | 7 pm | Midnight
check: tf | Pm means the morning. | false | Am is the morning. Pm is the afternoon and night.
check: mcq | Midday is… | 12 o'clock in the middle of the day* | 12 o'clock at night | 6 pm
home: Write down three things you do in the am and three in the pm.
skill: time

@lesson 2.5 | How long? | ⏱️ | home
hook: The movie starts at 2:00 and ends at 3:30. How long is it?
learn: To find how long, count from the start time to the end time.
learn: 2:00 to 3:00 is 1 hour. 3:00 to 3:30 is 30 minutes more.
learn: So the movie is one and a half hours long.
try: 9:00 to 9:30 is 30 minutes :: gap | 4:00 to 4:15 is _ minutes | 15
play: gen | time | 3
check: gap | 10:00 to 11:00 is _ hour | 1
check: mcq | Swimming starts at 3:00 and ends at 3:45. How long is it? | 45 minutes* | 3 hours | 15 minutes
check: tf | 1:00 to 2:30 is one and a half hours. | true
home: Check when your favourite show starts and ends. How long is it?
skill: time

@lesson 2.6 | Being on time | 🚌 | home
hook: The school bus comes at 8:15. You're ready at 8:20. Uh oh!
learn: Being on time means being ready before it starts.
learn: Look at the clock and plan to be a few minutes early.
learn: If you might be late, tell a grown-up.
play: timing | Tap when the clock reaches bus time | Catch the bus! | 8:15
check: mcq | The bus comes at 8:15. When should you be ready? | 8:10* | 8:20 | 9:00
check: tf | Being ready a few minutes early helps you be on time. | true
check: mcq | Your swimming lesson is at 4:00. Which time is late? | 4:10* | 3:55 | 3:50
home: Pick something you go to each week. Plan what time you need to be ready.
skill: punctuality
