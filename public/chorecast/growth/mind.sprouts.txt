@lesson 1.1 | Naming feelings | 😊 | garden
hook: Feelings are like weather inside you. What's your weather today?
learn: All feelings are OK. Naming them helps you understand them.
learn: Happy, sad, worried, angry and excited are all feelings.
play: match | Match the feeling to when you might feel it | Excited=The night before your birthday; Worried=Before a test; Sad=When a friend moves away; Proud=When you finish something hard
check: mcq | Are all feelings OK to have? | Yes* | No | Only happy ones
check: tf | Naming a feeling can help you understand it. | true
check: mcq | How might you feel before a test? | Worried* | Sleepy | Hungry
home: At dinner, share one feeling you had today.
skill: feelings

@lesson 1.2 | Calm-down breathing | 🌬️ | garden
hook: When you're cross, your body gets hot and fast. Can breathing slow it down?
learn: Slow breathing tells your body to calm down.
learn: Try "smell the flower, blow out the candle": breathe in through your nose, out through your mouth.
play: timing | Breathe in... and tap when the bubble reaches calm | Breathe out | Calm
check: mcq | What helps you calm down? | Slow breaths* | Shouting | Running into things
check: tf | Slow breathing can help your body relax. | true
check: mcq | "Smell the flower" means… | Breathe in through your nose* | Eat the flower | Sneeze
home: Try five slow breaths before bed tonight.
skill: calm

@lesson 1.3 | Sleep power | 😴 | home
hook: Why do you feel grumpy after a late night?
learn: Sleep helps your body grow and your brain remember.
learn: Kids your age need about 9 to 12 hours of sleep.
play: sort | Helps sleep, Stops sleep | A bath before bed=Helps sleep; Screens in bed=Stops sleep; A bedtime story=Helps sleep; Lollies at night=Stops sleep; A dark, quiet room=Helps sleep; Scary videos=Stops sleep | Does it help or stop sleep?
check: mcq | About how much sleep do kids your age need? | 9 to 12 hours* | 3 hours | 20 hours
check: tf | Screens in bed can make it harder to sleep. | true
check: mcq | Sleep helps your brain… | Remember things* | Forget everything | Shrink
home: Make a bedtime routine with a grown-up.
skill: sleep

@lesson 1.4 | Walt's first try | 🎬 | museum
hook: A famous storyteller's first business failed. Did he give up?
learn: Walt Disney's first animation studio, Laugh-O-Gram, went broke in 1923.
learn: He moved, started again, and kept learning from mistakes.
play: path | Your first big project doesn't work out. | Learn what went wrong and try again => good: That's what Walt did, and his next studio became famous. | Quit forever => bad: Then you'd never find out what you could do. | Blame everyone else => ok: It's normal to feel upset, but learning from it helps more.
check: mcq | What did Walt Disney do after his first studio failed? | Started again* | Gave up | Hid
check: tf | Mistakes can help you learn. | true
check: mcq | Laugh-O-Gram was… | Walt's first studio* | A cartoon mouse | A theme park
home: Try something again that didn't work the first time.
skill: grit
