@lesson 1.4 | Washing up | 🍽️ | kitchen
hook: Glasses, plates or greasy pots. Which go in the water first?

play: order | Put the washing-up in order | Scrape scraps into the bin; Fill the sink with hot soapy water; Wash glasses; Wash cutlery; Wash plates; Wash pots last

check: tf | Washing pots last keeps the water cleaner for longer. | true
check: mcq | Before washing, you should… | Scrape scraps off* | Add more scraps | Nothing
home: Help wash the dishes in the right order tonight.

@lesson 2.1 | Hot, hot, hot | 🔥 | kitchen
hook: The pot is bubbling on the stove. How do you stay safe?
learn: Stoves, ovens, kettles and toasters get very hot.
learn: Turn pot handles in, away from the edge, so nobody knocks them.
learn: Use oven mitts, and stay back unless a grown-up is helping.

@lesson 2.2 | Sharp things | 🔪 | kitchen
hook: Knives make cooking easier, but they can cut you. What are the knife rules?
learn: Only use a knife when a grown-up says it's OK and is watching.
learn: Carry a knife with the point down, by your side.
learn: Never drop a knife into a sink full of bubbles. Someone might reach in without seeing it.
play: order | Pass a knife to a grown-up safely | Ask if they're ready; Hold it by the handle; Keep the point down; Put it on the bench in front of them; Let them pick it up

check: tf | It's safe to drop a knife into a sink full of bubbles. | false | Someone could reach in and get cut.
check: mcq | When can you use a knife? | When a grown-up says OK and is watching* | Whenever you like | When nobody is home

@lesson 2.5 | Raw and ready | 🍗 | kitchen

@lesson 2.6 | If you get burnt | 🚿 | kitchen
hook: Ouch! You touched a hot pan. What should you do?
learn: Put the burn under cool running water for 20 minutes.
learn: Tell a grown-up straight away.
learn: Don't put butter, ice or toothpaste on a burn. Cool water is best.
play: order | Put the burn first aid steps in order | Move away from the hot thing; Tell a grown-up; Put the burn under cool running water; Keep it there for 20 minutes; Let a grown-up check if you need a doctor
check: mcq | What's the best first aid for a small burn? | Cool running water for 20 minutes* | Butter | Ice
check: tf | You should tell a grown-up if you get burnt. | true
check: mcq | How long should a burn stay under cool water? | 20 minutes* | 2 seconds | All day
home: Show a grown-up what you would do for a small burn.
