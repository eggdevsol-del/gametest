@lesson 1.3 | Water safety | 🏊 | home
hook: The beach looks fun. What keeps you safe?
learn: Always swim with a grown-up watching.
learn: At the beach, swim between the red and yellow flags.
play: order | Get ready to swim safely | Find a grown-up to watch; Look for the red and yellow flags; Swim between the flags; Stay close to the shore; Get out if you feel tired or cold
check: mcq | Where should you swim at the beach? | Between the red and yellow flags* | Wherever you like | Far out
check: tf | You can swim alone if you're a good swimmer. | false | Always have a grown-up watching.
check: mcq | You feel tired in the water. You should… | Get out and rest* | Swim further | Go under
home: Next time you swim, find the flags with a grown-up.
skill: safety

@lesson 1.4 | Washing up | 🍽️ | kitchen
hook: Glasses, plates or greasy pots. Which go in the water first?
learn: Wash the cleanest things first: glasses, then cutlery, then plates, then pots.
learn: That way the water stays cleaner for longer.
play: order | Put the washing-up in order | Scrape scraps into the bin; Fill the sink with hot soapy water; Wash glasses; Wash cutlery; Wash plates; Wash pots last
check: mcq | What goes in the water first? | Glasses* | Greasy pots | Dirty pans
check: tf | Washing pots last keeps the water cleaner for longer. | true
check: mcq | Before washing, you should… | Scrape scraps off* | Add more scraps | Nothing
home: Help wash the dishes in the right order tonight.
skill: chores

@lesson 1.5 | Folding clothes | 👕 | home
hook: Can you fold a t-shirt so it fits in a drawer?
learn: Lay it flat. Fold each side in. Then fold it in half.
learn: Folded clothes stay neat and are easy to find.
play: build | Fold the t-shirt in order | Lay the t-shirt flat; Fold the left side in; Fold the right side in; Fold it in half from the bottom; Put it in the drawer
check: mcq | First step to fold a t-shirt? | Lay it flat* | Throw it | Scrunch it
check: tf | Folded clothes are easier to find. | true
check: mcq | Where do folded t-shirts go? | In the drawer* | On the floor | In the bath
home: Fold three t-shirts on your own.
skill: chores
