@lesson 1.1 | What stress does | 😰 | garden
hook: Exams, sport, friends. Why does your heart race before a test?
learn: Stress is your body's alarm. A little can help you focus. Too much for too long wears you down.
learn: Sleep, movement, breaking tasks into steps and talking help.
play: sort | Helpful, Unhelpful | Breaking study into small chunks=Helpful; Pulling an all-nighter=Unhelpful; A walk outside=Helpful; Energy drinks all day=Unhelpful; Talking to a friend=Helpful; Doom-scrolling at midnight=Unhelpful | Helpful or unhelpful for stress?
check: mcq | What is stress? | Your body's alarm system* | A type of food | Always bad
check: tf | A little stress can help you focus. | true
check: mcq | A good way to handle a big task? | Break it into small steps* | Avoid it until the last minute | Panic
home: Break one big task this week into three small steps.
skill: stress

@lesson 1.2 | Peer pressure | 🫂 | garden
hook: Your friends want to skip class. You don't. What do you say?
learn: Peer pressure is feeling pushed to do what others do.
learn: Have a few lines ready: "Nah, I'm good." "I've got stuff on." Real friends respect your no.
play: chat | Friends want you to skip class | them: Come on, let's skip last period. Everyone's going. | me: Nah, I'm good. I've got a test to study for*/Fine, whatever you say/I'll do anything you want | them: Don't be boring. | me: Have fun. Catch you after school*/OK, I'll come/You're all losers
check: mcq | What's peer pressure? | Feeling pushed to do what others do* | A type of exercise | A school rule
check: tf | Real friends respect your choices. | true
check: mcq | A good way to say no? | Short and confident: "Nah, I'm good"* | A long angry speech | Saying yes anyway
home: Practise one "no thanks" line out loud.
skill: peers

@lesson 1.3 | Ads and feelings | 📺 | online
hook: Why do ads show happy friends instead of just the product?
learn: Ads link products to feelings like belonging, fun or looking cool.
learn: Influencers are often paid. Look for "ad", "sponsored" or "#gifted".
play: tap | Tap the ad tricks | 😎:yes:Cool people make you want to be like them; ⏳:yes:"Limited time!" makes you rush; ⭐:yes:Celebrity endorsements borrow their fame; 📏:no; 🆓:yes:"Free" offers often have a catch; 📖:no
check: mcq | Ads often link products to… | Feelings like fun or belonging* | Boring facts only | Nothing
check: tf | Influencers are sometimes paid to post. | true
check: mcq | What should paid posts show? | Ad, sponsored or gifted* | Nothing | A secret code
home: Find an ad and name the feeling it's selling.
skill: advertising

@lesson 1.4 | Emotional spending | 🛍️ | stall
hook: Bad day, so you buy something to feel better. Does it work?
learn: Emotional spending is buying to change your mood. The good feeling usually fades fast.
learn: Try a pause: wait 24 hours before non-essential buys.
play: path | You've had a rough day and see a $60 hoodie online. | Add it to a wishlist and wait 24 hours => good: Tomorrow you decide you don't really need it. $60 saved. | Buy it straight away => ok: It feels good for a bit, but your goal fund shrinks. | Buy two to feel extra better => bad: Double the cost, and the feeling still fades.
check: mcq | What is emotional spending? | Buying things to change your mood* | Buying food | Saving
check: tf | Waiting 24 hours can reduce impulse buys. | true
check: mcq | A non-spending way to feel better? | A walk or talking to a friend* | Buying more | Scrolling shops
home: Try the 24-hour rule on your next want.
skill: spending

@lesson 1.5 | Edited bodies | 🪞 | online
hook: That perfect photo online. Is it real?
learn: Many images are posed, filtered or edited.
learn: Comparing yourself to edited images can make you feel bad. Bodies come in all shapes and sizes.
play: tf | Most photos you see online are unedited. | false | Many are filtered, posed or edited.
check: mcq | What can make photos look "perfect"? | Filters, lighting and editing* | Nothing | Magic
check: tf | Healthy bodies come in many shapes and sizes. | true
check: mcq | A helpful habit? | Unfollow accounts that make you feel bad* | Compare yourself all day | Edit every photo
home: Unfollow or mute one account that makes you feel worse.
skill: bodyimage
