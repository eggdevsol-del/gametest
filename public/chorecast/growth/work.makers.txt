@lesson 1.1 | Your first CV | 📄 | workshop
hook: You've never had a job. What goes on your CV?
learn: A CV lists your contact details, skills, experience and references.
learn: Experience can include volunteering, school leadership, sport and helping at home or for neighbours.
learn: Keep it to one or two pages and check the spelling.
play: sort | Put it on, Leave it off | Volunteering at the food bank=Put it on; Your favourite pizza=Leave it off; School sports captain=Put it on; Lawn-mowing for neighbours=Put it on; Your password=Leave it off; A reference from your coach=Put it on | What belongs on a first CV?
check: mcq | What counts as experience on a first CV? | Volunteering and school roles* | Only paid jobs | Nothing
check: tf | Spelling mistakes on a CV don't matter. | false
check: mcq | How long should a first CV be? | One or two pages* | Ten pages | One line
home: Start a CV draft with three things you've done that show responsibility.
skill: cv

@lesson 1.3 | Interviews | 🎤 | stage
hook: The interviewer asks, "Tell me about yourself." What do you say?
learn: Prepare 3 short stories that show your skills, like a time you solved a problem.
learn: Arrive early, dress neatly, make eye contact and ask a question at the end.

Tell me a bit about yourself. | me: I'm in Year 11, I play netball and I help run the school canteen on Fridays*/Um, I dunno/I need money | them: Great. Tell me about a time you dealt with a problem. | me: Once the canteen ran out of change, so I got smaller notes from the office and kept the line moving*/I never have problems/My friend did it | them: Any questions for us? | me: What does a typical shift look like?*/No/When do I get paid more?
check: mcq | What helps most in an interview? | Prepared examples of your skills* | Wearing sunglasses | Talking about money only
check: tf | Asking a question at the end shows interest. | true
check: mcq | When should you arrive? | A few minutes early* | Right on time or a bit late | Whenever
home: Practise answering "Tell me about yourself" with a grown-up.
skill: interview

@lesson 1.6 | Solving problems at work | 🧩 | workshop
hook: The till is $10 short at the end of your shift. What do you do?
learn: Stay calm. Check the facts first.
learn: Tell your supervisor honestly, even if it might be your mistake.
play: path | The till is $10 short at the end of your shift. | Count again and tell your supervisor => good: Honesty builds trust, and it was a miscounted $10 note. | Put in $10 of your own and say nothing => ok: Kind, but the business needs to know about mistakes so they can fix the cause. | Change the numbers so it looks right => bad: That's dishonest and could cost you your job.
check: mcq | Something goes wrong at work. First step? | Stay calm and check the facts* | Hide it | Blame someone else
check: tf | Being honest about mistakes builds trust. | true
check: mcq | Who should you tell about a till error? | Your supervisor* | Nobody | Customers
home: Think of a mistake you made and how you'd handle it now.

@lesson 2.1 | Minimum pay | 💵 | bank
hook: You and a 25-year-old do the same job at a café. Can you be paid less?
learn: {nz:NZ has one adult minimum wage. Some 16 to 19 year olds can be paid the starting-out rate, 80% of the adult rate, for example in their first 6 months with an employer.~au:Australia has a national minimum wage, and awards set minimum rates for each kind of job. Workers under 21 can be paid junior rates, a percentage of the adult rate.}
learn: Minimum rates usually go up each year. Check {nz:employment.govt.nz~au:fairwork.gov.au} for this year's numbers.
learn: Being paid below the legal minimum is never OK, even if you agreed to it.

check: mcq | {nz:What's the starting-out rate?~au:What are junior rates?} | {nz:80% of the adult minimum, for some 16 to 19 year olds~au:A percentage of the adult rate, for workers under 21}* | {nz:A bonus for new workers~au:A bonus for young workers} | {nz:Half the adult rate for everyone under 20~au:The same as the adult rate}

@lesson 2.2 | Breaks | 🥪 | workshop
hook: You've worked 4 hours straight with no break. Your manager says breaks are for adults. Right?
learn: {nz:In NZ, the law gives employees paid 10-minute rest breaks and unpaid 30-minute meal breaks, depending on how long they work.~au:In Australia, your award or agreement sets your breaks. Many give a paid rest break and an unpaid meal break on longer shifts.}
learn: {nz:For example, a 4 to 6 hour shift comes with one paid 10-minute rest break and one 30-minute meal break.~au:The Fair Work Ombudsman's online pay and conditions tool shows the breaks in your award.}
learn: Break rules cover young workers too.
play: path | You're 4 hours into a 6-hour shift with no break. It's busy. | Politely ask your manager when you can take your break => good: Fair and calm. Breaks are a right, and good managers plan for them. | Keep working and say nothing, every shift => bad: You'll get tired and make mistakes, and you miss out on what you're owed. | Storm out mid-shift => bad: Leaving without telling anyone causes real problems. Ask first.
check: tf | Young workers have no right to breaks. | false
check: mcq | {nz:In NZ, a 4 to 6 hour shift includes…~au:What sets your breaks in Australia?} | {nz:A paid 10-minute rest break and a 30-minute meal break~au:Your award or agreement}* | {nz:No breaks~au:Your manager's mood} | {nz:A 2-hour lunch~au:Nothing at all}
check: mcq | Why do breaks matter? | Tired workers make more mistakes and get hurt* | They don't | They're only for adults
home: Ask a grown-up what breaks they get at work.

@lesson 2.5 | Trial shifts | 🚫 | stall
hook: A café asks you to do a 6-hour unpaid "trial" shift serving customers. Fair?
learn: {nz:In NZ, if you're doing real work for a business, you should be paid for it, trial shifts included.~au:In Australia, an unpaid trial is only allowed if it's short, supervised and just to show your skills. A whole shift of real work should be paid.}
learn: Be wary of businesses that keep asking for free "trials" or "work experience" but never hire.

check: tf | A business can make you work a full shift for free as a "trial". | false
