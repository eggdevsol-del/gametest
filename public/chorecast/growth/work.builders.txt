@lesson 1.1 | So many jobs | 🗺️ | workshop
hook: There are thousands of different jobs. How many can you name in 30 seconds?
learn: Jobs fit into groups like health, building, technology, the outdoors, creative work and helping people.
learn: Many jobs need skills you can start building now.
play: sort | Health, Building, Technology, Outdoors | Nurse=Health; Builder=Building; App developer=Technology; Park ranger=Outdoors; Dentist=Health; Electrician=Building; Game designer=Technology; Farmer=Outdoors | Which group does each job fit?
check: mcq | Which job is in the health group? | Physiotherapist* | Plumber | Coder
check: tf | You can start building job skills while you're still at school. | true
check: mcq | Which job is outdoors? | Ranger* | Accountant | Software tester
home: Ask three grown-ups what their job is and what they like about it.
skill: careers

@lesson 1.2 | What employers want | 🧲 | workshop
hook: Two people apply for a job. One is brilliant but late. One is reliable and keen. Who gets it?
learn: Employers value reliability, attitude, teamwork and willingness to learn.
learn: These are called soft skills. They matter in every job.
play: sort | Soft skill, Not a soft skill | Turning up on time=Soft skill; Being polite to customers=Soft skill; Owning a fancy phone=Not a soft skill; Asking questions to learn=Soft skill; Having lots of followers=Not a soft skill; Working well in a team=Soft skill | Which are soft skills?
check: mcq | Which do employers value most in a new worker? | Reliability and a good attitude* | Expensive shoes | Being famous
check: tf | Soft skills only matter in some jobs. | false
check: mcq | What's a soft skill? | Teamwork* | Height | A big house
home: Name one soft skill you're good at and one to work on.
skill: softskills

@lesson 1.3 | Being reliable | ⏰ | home
hook: You promised to walk the neighbour's dog at 4pm. It's 3:55 and your show is on. What now?
learn: Reliable people do what they say, when they say.
learn: If you can't, you tell people early and make a plan.
play: path | You promised to walk the neighbour's dog at 4pm, but a friend invites you to the park. | Walk the dog first, then go to the park => good: You kept your promise and still had fun. That's reliable. | Go to the park and say nothing => bad: The dog waited. The neighbour may not trust you again. | Message the neighbour early to swap the time => ok: Telling people early is much better than not turning up.
check: mcq | What does reliable mean? | Doing what you say you'll do* | Doing what you feel like | Being fast
check: tf | If you can't do something, telling people early is better than not showing up. | true
check: mcq | Why does reliability matter for paid jobs? | People keep hiring you* | It doesn't | It makes jobs shorter
home: Make one promise this week and keep it exactly.
skill: reliability

@lesson 1.4 | Great customer service | 😊 | stall
hook: A customer at your stall is grumpy. What do you say?
learn: Good service means being polite, listening and helping.
learn: Happy customers come back and tell their friends.
play: chat | A customer is unhappy with their cupcake | them: This cupcake is squashed! | me: Oh no, sorry about that. Would you like a new one?*/Not my problem/You probably dropped it | them: Yes please. Thanks for being nice about it. | me: No worries. Enjoy, and come back soon!*/Whatever/Don't drop this one
check: mcq | A customer complains. What's the best first step? | Listen and say sorry* | Argue | Ignore them
check: tf | Happy customers can bring more customers. | true
check: mcq | What's good customer service? | Polite, helpful and listening* | Fast and grumpy | Rude but cheap
home: Practise saying "How can I help?" in a friendly voice.
skill: service

@lesson 1.5 | Taking feedback | 🗣️ | workshop
hook: Your boss says your work "could be better". Are you in trouble?
learn: Feedback helps you improve. It's about the work, not about you.
learn: Say thanks, ask how to fix it, then try again.
play: order | Put the steps for handling feedback in order | Listen without interrupting; Say thanks for the feedback; Ask how to do it better; Try again; Check if it's better now
check: mcq | Someone says your work needs fixing. Best response? | "Thanks. How can I make it better?"* | "You're wrong" | Walk away
check: tf | Feedback is about your work, not who you are. | true
check: mcq | What should you do after feedback? | Try again using it* | Forget it | Stop working
home: Ask a grown-up for one piece of feedback on a chore, and use it.
skill: feedback

@lesson 1.6 | Juggling time | 📆 | home
hook: Homework, sport, a pet-sitting job and a birthday party, all on Saturday. Help!
learn: Write everything down, then sort by what's important and what's urgent.
learn: Say no or move things if it's too much.
play: sort | Do first, Plan for later, Maybe skip | Homework due tomorrow=Do first; Pet-sitting you promised=Do first; Tidying your games shelf=Plan for later; Watching another episode=Maybe skip; Practising for next week's test=Plan for later; Scrolling videos=Maybe skip | Sort your Saturday
check: mcq | Too many things on one day. Good first step? | Write them all down* | Panic | Do none of them
check: tf | It's OK to say no if you have too much on. | true
check: mcq | What goes first? | Important things that are due soon* | Whatever's most fun | Nothing
home: Plan your next weekend on paper before it starts.
skill: planning
