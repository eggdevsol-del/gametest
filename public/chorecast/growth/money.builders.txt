@lesson 7.3 | Takings minus costs | 🧮 | stall
hook: You sold $40 of cupcakes. Did you make $40?
learn: Takings are all the money customers pay you.
learn: Costs are what you spent to make things, like flour, icing and cupcake cases.
learn: Profit is takings minus costs. That's what you really earned.
try: $40 takings − $12 costs = $28 profit :: gap | $30 takings − $9 costs = $_ profit | 21
play: slider | You sold 20 cupcakes at $2 each. The ingredients cost $14. What's your profit? | 0 | 40 | 26 | $ | 0 | 1
check: gap | $50 takings − $20 costs = $_ profit | 30
check: tf | Takings and profit are the same thing. | false | Profit is what's left after you pay your costs.
home: Work out the costs of something you could bake and sell, with a grown-up.
skill: profit
