@lesson 2.3 | Hardship help | 🤝 | bank
hook: You lose your job and can't make your loan repayment. Is it too late?
learn: Banks and lenders have hardship teams. Call as soon as you can, ideally before you miss a payment.
learn: They may lower repayments, pause them for a while or stretch the loan out.
learn: {nz:Under NZ lending law, you can apply for a hardship change if illness, job loss, a relationship ending or a similar event means you can't pay.~au:Under Australian credit law, you can ask your lender for a hardship change if you can't meet your repayments.}
learn: Power, water and phone companies have hardship help too.
play: order | You've lost your job and a repayment is due. Put the steps in order. | Work out what you can afford to pay; Call the lender's hardship team; Explain what's happened and what you can pay; Agree a new plan in writing; Get free help from a {nz:financial mentor~au:financial counsellor} if you need it
check: mcq | When should you contact your lender about hardship? | As soon as you know you'll struggle* | After three missed payments | Never
check: tf | Lenders may lower or pause repayments for a while. | true
check: mcq | Which events can count as hardship? | Job loss or illness* | Wanting a holiday | A new phone coming out
home: Find the hardship page on a bank's website.
skill: hardship

@lesson 2.4 | Debt traps | 🪤 | bank
hook: You're short $300. An ad says "Instant cash, no credit check!" What's the catch?
learn: Payday and other high-cost loans are fast, but very expensive.
learn: {nz:NZ caps high-cost loans, so the total interest and fees can't be more than the amount you borrowed.~au:Australia caps small loans of up to $2,000 at a 20% set-up fee plus 4% a month.}
learn: Even with caps, they can trap you if you borrow again to repay. Look for cheaper help first.
learn: {nz:Ask about no-interest loans from community lenders, or Work and Income for urgent costs.~au:Ask about No Interest Loans (NILs) from community lenders, or a Centrelink advance payment.}
play: price | You need $300. Which costs less in total? | Payday loan, repay $480 over a year:480:1:loan; No-interest community loan, repay $300:300:1:loan
check: mcq | What's a big risk with payday loans? | Borrowing again to repay, and getting trapped* | They're too cheap | They take years to arrive
check: tf | No-interest loans may be available from community lenders. | true
check: mcq | {nz:In NZ, the interest and fees on a high-cost loan can't be more than…~au:In Australia, small loans of up to $2,000 are capped at…} | {nz:The amount you borrowed~au:A 20% set-up fee plus 4% a month}* | {nz:$5~au:Nothing at all} | {nz:Ten times the loan~au:100% a day}
home: Look up {nz:Good Shepherd NZ's no-interest loans~au:Good Shepherd's No Interest Loans} with a grown-up.
