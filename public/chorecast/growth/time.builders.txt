@lesson 1.1 | Why money was invented | 💡 | museum
hook: Imagine needing to swap a cow for a pair of shoes. What goes wrong?
learn: Barter only works if both people want each other's things. That's called a "double coincidence of wants".
learn: Money solves it. It's a medium of exchange, a store of value and a unit of account.
play: match | Match each job of money to its meaning | Medium of exchange=Everyone accepts it for swaps; Store of value=You can save it for later; Unit of account=It lets you compare prices

check: tf | Money lets you compare the prices of different things. | true
check: mcq | "Store of value" means… | You can save it for later* | It's stored in a shop | It's heavy
home: Explain to someone why a cow is bad money.

@lesson 1.2 | Mansa Musa's gold | 👑 | museum

learn: With so much gold around, it was worth less for years. That's inflation.

@lesson 2.1 | The spice trade | 🌶️ | museum
hook: In Europe 600 years ago, pepper and nutmeg were so pricey that mostly the rich could afford them. Why?
learn: Nutmeg and cloves grew only on a few small islands in what is now Indonesia, often called the Spice Islands.
learn: Spices travelled thousands of kilometres by ship and camel, passing through many traders.
learn: Each trader added to the price, so spices were very expensive by the time they reached Europe.
