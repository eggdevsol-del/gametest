@lesson 1.1 | Why money was invented | 💡 | museum
hook: Imagine needing to swap a cow for a pair of shoes. What goes wrong?
learn: Barter only works if both people want each other's things. That's called a "double coincidence of wants".
learn: Money solves it. It's a medium of exchange, a store of value and a unit of account.
play: match | Match each job of money to its meaning | Medium of exchange=Everyone accepts it for swaps; Store of value=You can save it for later; Unit of account=It lets you compare prices
check: mcq | What problem does money solve? | Needing both people to want each other's things* | Too many shops | Too few cows
check: tf | Money lets you compare the prices of different things. | true
check: mcq | "Store of value" means… | You can save it for later* | It's stored in a shop | It's heavy
home: Explain to someone why a cow is bad money.
skill: history

@lesson 1.2 | Mansa Musa's gold | 👑 | museum
hook: The richest king in history gave away so much gold that its price crashed. How?
learn: Mansa Musa ruled the Mali Empire in West Africa in the 1300s.
learn: On a journey to Mecca in 1324, he gave away huge amounts of gold, especially in Cairo.
learn: With so much gold around, it was worth less for years. That's inflation.
play: mystery | He ruled the Mali Empire; He travelled to Mecca in 1324; He gave away so much gold its value fell in Cairo; He's often called the richest person ever | Mansa Musa* | Andrew Carnegie | The Medici
check: mcq | Why did gold lose value in Cairo? | There was suddenly much more of it* | It turned to sand | People stopped liking yellow
check: tf | When there's much more of something, it can be worth less. | true
check: mcq | What's that called with money? | Inflation* | Interest | Insurance
home: Think of something that got cheaper because there was lots of it.
skill: inflation

@lesson 1.4 | The first banks | 🏛️ | bank
hook: Why would you give your money to someone else to keep?
learn: Early banks kept people's money safe and lent some of it to others.
learn: Borrowers paid interest, and banks shared some with savers.
learn: Banks only work if people trust them.
play: order | Put the bank idea in order | Savers leave money at the bank; The bank keeps some safe; It lends some to a borrower; The borrower repays with interest; The bank pays savers a little interest

check: tf | Banks need people's trust to work. | true
check: mcq | What do borrowers pay? | Interest* | Nothing | A cow
home: Ask a grown-up why they trust their bank.
skill: banking

@lesson 1.5 | The first shareholders | ⛵ | museum
hook: Sailing ships to Asia was risky. How did merchants share the risk?
learn: In 1602 the Dutch East India Company sold shares to the public.
learn: Many people owned small pieces, so no one lost everything if a ship sank.
learn: Its shares were traded in Amsterdam, an early stock exchange.
play: mcq | Why did selling shares help? | Many people shared the risk and the profit* | Ships became unsinkable | Nobody had to pay
check: mcq | When did the Dutch East India Company sell shares? | 1602* | 1999 | 1066
check: tf | Shares let many people own a small part of a company. | true
check: mcq | Where were its shares traded? | Amsterdam* | Sydney | Auckland
home: Explain sharing risk using a bake sale with friends.
skill: shares

@lesson 1.6 | Money tricks of history | 🎭 | museum
hook: Kings once mixed cheap metal into silver coins. Why would that matter?
learn: When rulers made coins with less silver, the coins were worth less, and prices rose.
learn: People noticed and trusted the money less.
play: debate | Should a government make more money when it needs to pay bills? | Printing too much can make prices rise, so it must be careful* | Yes, just print as much as needed | Some extra money can help in emergencies. The risk is doing too much.
check: mcq | What happens when money is made with less silver? | It's worth less and prices rise* | It's worth more | Nothing
check: tf | Trust matters for money to work. | true
check: mcq | Too much money chasing the same goods causes… | Inflation* | Free lunches | Interest
home: Find a price that has gone up over the years.
skill: inflation
