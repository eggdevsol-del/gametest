@lesson 1.1 | Kind acts | 💛 | garden
hook: Can one small kind thing really change someone's day?
learn: Kindness is doing something nice for someone, even when you don't have to.
learn: It can be a smile, a helping hand or sharing a toy.
play: sort | Kind, Not kind | Holding the door open=Kind; Saying thank you=Kind; Laughing at someone who fell=Not kind; Sharing your crayons=Kind; Leaving someone out of a game=Not kind; Helping pick up dropped books=Kind | Is it kind?
check: mcq | Which is a kind act? | Helping a friend carry their bag* | Taking their snack | Ignoring them
check: tf | Kindness has to cost money. | false
check: mcq | How does kindness make people feel? | Happier* | Sadder | Nothing
home: Do three kind things today and tell a grown-up what they were.
skill: kindness

@lesson 1.2 | What does giving mean? | 🎁 | garden

learn: You can give money, time, things or kindness.

check: mcq | Which is giving time? | Helping at a clean-up* | Buying a toy for yourself | Watching TV
check: tf | You can give without money. | true
check: mcq | What could you give from your toy box? | Toys you don't play with any more* | Toys that are broken and dirty | Your sibling's toys without asking
home: Find one thing you've outgrown that someone else could use.
skill: giving

@lesson 2.1 | What is sharing? | 🤲 | garden
hook: You have 6 strawberries. Your friend has none. What could you do?
learn: Sharing means letting others use or have some of what you have.
learn: Sharing can make you and the other person feel good.
learn: You can share things, your time and kind words.
try: 6 strawberries shared by 2 is 3 each :: gap | 8 grapes shared by 2 is _ each | 4
play: sort | Sharing, Not sharing | Giving a friend half your sandwich=Sharing; Taking all the crayons=Not sharing; Letting someone have a go of your game=Sharing; Hiding the ball=Not sharing; Reading a story to your little sister=Sharing; Grabbing the last biscuit=Not sharing | Is it sharing?
check: mcq | 10 grapes shared between 2 friends is… | 5 each* | 10 each | 1 each
check: tf | You can share time and kind words, not just things. | true
check: mcq | How can sharing feel? | Good for you and your friend* | Always bad | Scary
home: Share something with someone in your family today.
skill: sharing

@lesson 2.2 | Taking turns | 🔄 | garden
hook: There is one swing and three kids. What's fair?
learn: Taking turns is a kind of sharing.
learn: Everyone gets a go. Counting or a timer can make it fair.
learn: Waiting for your turn is hard, but it's fair.
play: order | Three kids, one swing. Put a fair plan in order. | Agree to take turns; Pick who goes first; Count to 50 for each turn; Swap when the count ends; Start again with the first kid
check: mcq | What is a fair way to share one swing? | Take turns* | The biggest kid keeps it | Nobody uses it
check: tf | A timer can help make turns fair. | true
check: mcq | Your friend has had a long turn. What could you say? | Can I have the next turn, please?* | Get off now! | Nothing, and push them
home: Play a game with your family where everyone takes turns.
skill: sharing

@lesson 2.3 | Fair shares | 🍕 | kitchen
hook: Four friends and one pizza. How do you cut it so it's fair?
learn: A fair share means everyone gets the same amount.
learn: Cut a pizza into 4 equal slices, and each friend gets 1.
learn: If someone is extra hungry, you can talk about it together.
try: 8 slices shared by 4 is 2 each :: gap | 12 slices shared by 4 is _ each | 3
play: slider | 20 crackers shared fairly by 4 kids. How many each? | 1 | 10 | 5 | crackers | 0 | 1
check: gap | 6 cookies shared by 3 is _ each | 2
check: mcq | What does a fair share mean? | Everyone gets the same* | The fastest gets the most | Grown-ups get none
check: tf | Talking together helps when sharing is tricky. | true
home: Help share out a snack fairly in your family.
skill: fairness
