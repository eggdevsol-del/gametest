@lesson 1.1 | Kind acts | 💛 | garden
hook: Can one small kind thing really change someone's day?
learn: Kindness is doing something nice for someone, even when you don't have to.
learn: It can be a smile, a helping hand or sharing a toy.
play: sort | Kind, Not kind | Holding the door open=Kind; Saying thank you=Kind; Laughing at someone who fell=Not kind; Sharing your crayons=Kind; Leaving someone out of a game=Not kind; Helping pick up dropped books=Kind | Is it kind?
check: mcq | Which is a kind act? | Helping a friend carry their bag* | Taking their snack | Ignoring them
check: tf | Kindness has to cost money. | false
check: mcq | How does kindness make people feel? | Happier* | Sadder | Nothing
home: Do three kind things today and tell a grown-up what they were.
skill: kindness

@lesson 1.2 | What does giving mean? | 🎁 | garden
hook: Can you give without spending money?
learn: Giving means sharing what you have to help others.
learn: You can give money, time, things or kindness.
play: match | Match each kind of giving to an example | Money=Coins from your Give jar for the shelter; Time=Helping at a beach clean-up; Things=Toys you've outgrown; Kindness=Making a card for someone who's sick
check: mcq | Which is giving time? | Helping at a clean-up* | Buying a toy for yourself | Watching TV
check: tf | You can give without money. | true
check: mcq | What could you give from your toy box? | Toys you don't play with any more* | Toys that are broken and dirty | Your sibling's toys without asking
home: Find one thing you've outgrown that someone else could use.
skill: giving

@lesson 1.3 | What do you care about? | 🌏 | garden
hook: Animals, people, or the planet. Which would you help first?
learn: A cause is something you want to help.
learn: Picking a cause you care about makes giving feel good.
play: mcq | Which cause do you care about most right now? | Animals* | People who need food or a home* | Nature and the planet* | Kids at my school*
check: mcq | What is a cause? | Something you want to help* | A type of toy | A shop
check: tf | It's good to pick causes you care about. | true
check: mcq | An animal shelter helps… | Pets that need homes* | Cars | Computers
home: Tell a grown-up which cause you'd pick and why.
skill: giving

@lesson 1.4 | Filling the Give jar | 🫙 | bank
hook: Your Give jar has 50c. Can tiny amounts help?
learn: Your Give jar grows a little every payday.
learn: Lots of small amounts add up to a big help.
try: 10c each week for 10 weeks is $1 :: gap | 20c each week for 10 weeks = $_ | 2
play: sort | jars | Saving to help the animal shelter=Give; A new toy=Spend; Money for later=Vault; Shares that might grow=Invest; Buying food for the food bank=Give | Which jar?
check: tf | Small amounts can add up to a big help. | true
check: mcq | Which jar is for helping others? | Give* | Spend | Invest
check: gap | 50c a week for 4 weeks = $_ | 2
home: Look at your Give jar and decide who it might help.
skill: giving

@lesson 1.5 | Ryan's well | 💧 | garden
hook: A 6-year-old heard some kids had to walk hours for clean water. What did he do?
learn: In 1998, Ryan Hreljac from Canada learned that a well could bring clean water to a village.
learn: He did extra chores to earn the money, then kept going.
learn: His idea grew into a charity that has helped build many wells.
play: order | Put Ryan's story in order | Ryan heard about kids without clean water; He did extra chores to earn money; He raised enough for his first well; People heard and helped too; It grew into a charity helping many communities
check: mcq | How did Ryan raise his first money? | Doing extra chores* | Winning a prize | Asking strangers online
check: tf | Kids can help make big changes. | true
check: mcq | What did Ryan's wells bring? | Clean water* | Toys | Cars
home: Think of a chore you could do to raise money for a cause.
skill: giving

@lesson 1.6 | Helping nature | 🌳 | garden

check: tf | Planting trees helps nature. | true
check: mcq | Who should you go with to a clean-up? | A grown-up* | Nobody | A stranger
home: Pick up three bits of rubbish (with gloves and a grown-up).
