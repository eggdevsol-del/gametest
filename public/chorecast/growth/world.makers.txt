@lesson 1.1 | Elections | 🗳️ | townhall
hook: Why does your vote matter if millions of people vote?
learn: {nz:New Zealand uses MMP. You get two votes: one for a local MP and one for a party.~au:Australia uses preferential voting for the House of Representatives. You number the candidates in order of preference.}
learn: {nz:General elections are held at least every three years.~au:Voting is compulsory for citizens 18 and over.}
learn: Close results happen. Every vote counts.
play: mcq | {nz:How many votes do you get in a NZ general election?~au:How do you fill in an Australian House of Representatives ballot?} | {nz:Two: an electorate vote and a party vote~au:Number every candidate in order of preference}* | {nz:One~au:Tick one name only} | {nz:Ten~au:Write a letter}
check: tf | {nz:NZ elections happen at least every three years.~au:Voting is compulsory for Australian citizens 18 and over.} | true
check: mcq | {nz:What voting system does NZ use?~au:What does "preferential" mean?} | {nz:MMP~au:You rank candidates in order}* | {nz:First past the post only~au:You pick your favourite colour} | {nz:Coin toss~au:Only one person votes}
check: mcq | Why does every vote matter? | Close results can be decided by a few votes* | It doesn't | Only famous people vote
home: Look up {nz:vote.nz~au:aec.gov.au} and find when the next election is.

@lesson 1.5 | Inflation and you | 🎈 | bank
hook: Why does the same bag of groceries cost more each year?
learn: Inflation means prices rise over time. Central banks try to keep it low and steady.
learn: If your pay or savings grow slower than inflation, you can buy less.
play: slider | Inflation is 4% a year. Roughly what does a $100 shop cost after 3 years? | 100 | 130 | 112 | $ | 3 | 1
check: mcq | What is inflation? | Prices rising over time* | Prices always falling | A balloon festival
check: tf | High inflation can make savings buy less. | true
check: mcq | Who tries to keep inflation steady? | The central bank* | Supermarkets | Schools
home: Compare a receipt from a year ago with today's prices.
skill: inflation
