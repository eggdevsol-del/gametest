@lesson 2.5 | Surplus, deficit and debt | 📊 | townhall
hook: A government spends $100 billion but only collects $90 billion. Where does the rest come from?
learn: When a government spends more than it collects in a year, that's a deficit. When it collects more, that's a surplus.
learn: Deficits are covered by borrowing, which adds to government debt.
learn: Debt isn't always bad, like borrowing after a disaster or to build hospitals. But the interest has to be paid every year.
play: diff | Two years of a government's budget. Tap what changed. | Tax in: $90b; Spending: $85b; Result: $5b surplus; Debt: $50b | Tax in: $90b; Spending: $100b; Result: $10b deficit; Debt: $60b
check: mcq | Spending $100b and collecting $90b gives a… | $10b deficit* | $10b surplus | $190b surplus
check: tf | Deficits are usually covered by borrowing. | true
check: mcq | What's one cost of government debt? | Interest that has to be paid every year* | Nothing at all | Free money
home: Find out whether the government expects a surplus or a deficit this year.
