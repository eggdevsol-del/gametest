@lesson 1.1 | Where money comes from | 💼 | bank

check: tf | A bank machine makes new money for anyone who asks. | false | It only gives out money that is already in your account.
home: Do one of your Chorecast chores this week. That's you earning money, just like a grown-up at work.

try: You have $10. Lunch costs $6. You have $4 left for a want :: gap | You have $8. A need costs $5. You have $_ left. | 3
play: path | You have $5. You need a drink bottle for school. It costs $4. You also want a toy car for $5. | Buy the drink bottle first => good: Great choice. Your need is sorted, and you have $1 to start saving for the car. | Buy the toy car => bad: Fun for now, but you will be thirsty at school with no bottle. | Ask for both and get cross => bad: Getting cross doesn't make more money. Picking needs first does.
check: mcq | What should you pay for first? | Needs* | Wants | The shiniest thing
