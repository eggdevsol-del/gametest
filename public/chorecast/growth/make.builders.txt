@lesson 1.1 | How much stock? | 📦 | stall
hook: You bake 100 cookies for the school fair and sell 30. What went wrong?
learn: Stock is what you have ready to sell.
learn: Too much and you waste money. Too little and you sell out early.
learn: Use last time's sales to guess, then add a little extra.
play: slider | Last fair you sold 40 cookies and ran out an hour early. How many should you bake this time? | 20 | 100 | 50 | cookies | 8 | 1
check: mcq | What happens if you make too much stock? | You might waste money* | You always earn more | Nothing
check: tf | Last time's sales help you plan this time. | true
check: mcq | You sold out fast. Next time you should… | Make a bit more* | Make less | Stop selling
home: Plan how many treats you'd bake for a family event.
skill: planning

@lesson 1.4 | Money in, money out | 📒 | bank
hook: You sold $40 of cupcakes. Did you make $40?
learn: Takings are all the money that comes in.
learn: Profit is takings minus all costs, including ingredients, cases and the stall fee.

check: gap | Takings $50, costs $22. Profit = $_ | 28
check: tf | Takings and profit are the same thing. | false
check: mcq | Which is a cost? | The stall fee* | A customer's payment | A tip

@lesson 2.1 | What is marketing? | 📣 | stall
hook: You make the best brownies in town. Nobody knows. Do you sell any?
learn: Marketing is how you tell people about what you sell and why they'd want it.
learn: It covers the product, the price, where you sell it and how you tell people.
learn: These are often called the four Ps: product, price, place and promotion.

check: mcq | What is marketing? | Telling the right people why they'd want your product* | Only making posters | Buying things
check: tf | Price is part of marketing. | true
check: mcq | Which one is promotion? | A poster about your stall* | The flour in your brownies | Your cash box
home: Look at one ad and find its product, price, place and promotion.
skill: marketing

@lesson 2.4 | Honest marketing | ✅ | townhall

@lesson 2.5 | Word of mouth | 🗣️ | stall
hook: What makes people tell their friends about something?
learn: Word of mouth is when happy customers tell other people about you.
learn: It's free, and people trust friends more than ads.
learn: Great products and friendly service get people talking.
play: path | A customer says your brownie was a bit dry. | Thank them, give a fresh one, and tweak your recipe => good: They'll likely tell friends you're great to deal with. | Argue that it was fine => bad: They'll tell friends you were rude. | Ignore them => bad: A quiet, unhappy customer still tells others.
check: mcq | What is word of mouth? | Customers telling others about you* | Shouting through a megaphone | A paid ad
check: tf | People often trust a friend more than an ad. | true
check: mcq | What gets good word of mouth? | Great products and friendly service* | Rude replies | High prices
home: Ask a grown-up which local shop they'd recommend, and why.
skill: service
