@lesson 1.1 | Reading an employment agreement | 📑 | workshop
hook: You're handed a 6-page agreement and asked to sign today. Do you?
learn: You can take time to read it and get advice before signing.
learn: Check pay rate, hours, role, trial period, notice and leave.
learn: If something's unclear, ask before you sign, not after.
play: diff | The job ad and the agreement. Tap what doesn't match. | Pay: $25.00 an hour; Hours: 20 a week; Role: Barista; Start: 1 March | Pay: $23.00 an hour; Hours: 20 a week; Role: Barista and cleaner; Start: 1 March
check: mcq | You're asked to sign an agreement on the spot. Best move? | Ask for time to read it and get advice* | Sign without reading | Refuse the job
check: tf | Things that don't match the ad are worth asking about. | true
check: mcq | Which should an agreement include? | Pay rate, hours and duties* | Your favourite food | Your bank PIN
home: Ask a grown-up what's in their employment agreement.
skill: contracts

@lesson 1.2 | Pay types | 💵 | bank
hook: Hourly, salary, commission, casual. Which is which?
learn: Hourly workers are paid for each hour worked. Salaried workers get a fixed amount a year.
learn: Casual work can be flexible but less certain. Commission pays a share of what you sell.
play: match | Match the pay type to how it works | Hourly=Paid for each hour worked; Salary=A fixed amount per year; Commission=A share of what you sell; Casual=Work offered as needed, hours vary
check: mcq | Which pay type changes most week to week? | Casual or commission* | Salary | None
check: gap | $24 an hour × 25 hours = $_ | 600
check: tf | Salaried workers are paid the same each pay period. | true
home: Work out your weekly pay if you earned $23 an hour for 12 hours.
skill: payslip

@lesson 1.5 | Working for yourself | 🧾 | stall
hook: You start a lawn-mowing business. Who pays your tax?
learn: Self-employed people must keep records and pay their own tax.
learn: {nz:You may need to file an IR3 return and pay provisional tax as you earn more. GST registration is needed once turnover passes $60,000 a year.~au:You may need an ABN, and must lodge a tax return. GST registration is needed once turnover reaches $75,000 a year.}
learn: Set money aside from every payment for tax.

check: tf | Keeping receipts for business costs is important. | true
check: mcq | A smart habit for self-employed people? | Set aside money for tax from every payment* | Spend it all | Never send invoices

@lesson 1.6 | Speaking up | 📢 | townhall
