@lesson 2.1 | Markup or margin? | 📐 | stall
hook: You buy a candle for $6 and sell it for $10. Is that 40% or 67%?
learn: Markup compares profit to cost. $4 profit on a $6 cost is about a 67% markup.
learn: Margin compares profit to price. $4 profit on a $10 price is a 40% margin.
learn: Same sale, two numbers. Always say which one you mean.
try: Cost $5, price $10. Markup = $5 ÷ $5 = 100% :: gap | Cost $8, price $10. Markup = _% | 25
play: match | Match each sale to its markup | Cost $5, price $10=100%; Cost $10, price $15=50%; Cost $4, price $5=25%; Cost $2, price $8=300%
check: gap | Cost $20, price $25. Markup = _% | 25
check: mcq | Cost $6, price $10. What's the margin? | 40%* | 67% | 60%
check: tf | Markup and margin are always the same number. | false | Markup divides by cost. Margin divides by price.
home: Find something you could resell. Work out its markup and margin.
