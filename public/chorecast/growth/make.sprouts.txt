@lesson 1.2 | Costs | 🧾 | stall
hook: Lemons, sugar and cups cost money. Does that matter if you sell lemonade?
learn: Costs are what you spend to make your product.
learn: You need to sell for more than your costs.
play: order | Put the lemonade business in order | Buy lemons, sugar and cups; Make the lemonade; Sell cups to customers; Count the money; Take away what you spent
check: mcq | What are costs? | Money you spend to make things* | Money customers give you | Free stuff
check: tf | Lemons and cups are costs. | true
check: mcq | If a cup costs 30c to make, you should sell it for… | More than 30c* | Less than 30c | Nothing
home: Find the price of a bag of lemons at the shop.
skill: costs

@lesson 1.3 | Price and profit | 💰 | stall
hook: You sell a cup for 50c. It cost 30c to make. How much did you really earn?
learn: Price is what customers pay.
learn: Profit is price take away cost.
try: Price 50c take away cost 30c = 20c profit :: gap | Price 80c take away cost 30c = _c profit | 50
play: shop | Set a price for your lemonade | Lemonade | 30 | 300
check: gap | Price $1 take away cost 40c = _c profit | 60
check: tf | If the price is too high, fewer people buy. | true
check: mcq | What is profit? | Price take away costs* | All the money in the box | The cost of lemons
home: Work out the profit if you sold 10 cups at 50c that cost 30c each.
skill: profit
