@lesson 1.1 | Training smart | 💪 | garden
hook: How do athletes get fitter without getting hurt?
learn: Fitness has parts: heart and lung fitness (running, cycling), strength (push-ups, squats) and flexibility (stretching).
learn: Warm up before exercise and cool down after. Build up slowly instead of doing too much at once.
learn: Rest days let muscles repair and get stronger.
try: A warm-up gets your muscles ready :: mcq | Why have rest days? | Muscles repair and get stronger* | Rest is lazy | They make you slower
play: sort | Strength, Heart and lungs, Flexibility | Squats=Strength; Cycling=Heart and lungs; Stretching=Flexibility; Push-ups=Strength; Swimming laps=Heart and lungs; Yoga stretches=Flexibility | Which kind of fitness?
check: tf | Doing far too much exercise too soon can cause injuries. | true
check: mcq | What should you do before hard exercise? | Warm up* | Eat a huge meal | Nothing
check: mcq | Teens should be active for at least… | an hour a day* | an hour a month | 5 minutes a week
home: Plan a week of activity with a strength day, a stretch day and a rest day.
skill: fitness

@lesson 1.2 | Teen sleep | 🌙 | home
hook: Why do teenagers often feel wide awake at night and sleepy in the morning?
learn: Teens need about 8 to 10 hours of sleep a night.
learn: In the teenage years, the body clock shifts later, so it's harder to feel sleepy early.
learn: Helpful habits: the same wake-up time every day, morning daylight, no caffeine after lunch, and screens off well before bed.
try: Morning light helps reset your body clock :: mcq | How much sleep do teens need? | About 8 to 10 hours* | About 5 hours | About 14 hours
play: sort | Helps sleep, Hurts sleep | The same wake-up time every day=Helps sleep; An energy drink in the afternoon=Hurts sleep; Morning sunlight=Helps sleep; Scrolling in bed=Hurts sleep; A cool, dark room=Helps sleep | Which is it?
check: tf | A teenager's body clock tends to shift later. | true
check: mcq | Which can make it harder to fall asleep? | Caffeine late in the day* | Morning sunlight | A cool room
check: mcq | Sleep helps you… | learn, grow and stay well* | only feel lazy | nothing much
home: Keep a sleep diary for a week. Are you getting 8 to 10 hours?
skill: teen-sleep

@lesson 1.3 | Sun and water safety | 🏖️ | island
hook: Why can you get sunburnt on a cloudy day?
learn: UV rays from the sun burn skin and can cause skin cancer later in life. They pass through cloud.
learn: Protect yourself: {nz:slip, slop, slap and wrap~au:slip, slop, slap, seek and slide}. Put sunscreen on again every two hours and after swimming.
learn: In the water, swim between the flags at patrolled beaches, never swim alone, and check conditions first. If you're caught in a rip, stay calm, float and raise your hand for help.
try: Swim between the flags :: mcq | Can you get sunburnt on a cloudy day? | Yes, UV passes through cloud* | No, never | Only at night
play: sort | Safe, Not safe | Swimming between the flags=Safe; Swimming alone at a quiet beach=Not safe; Putting sunscreen on again after a swim=Safe; Diving into water when you can't see the bottom=Not safe; Wearing a hat at midday=Safe | Safe or not?
check: mcq | If you're caught in a rip, you should… | stay calm, float and raise your hand* | swim straight against it until you're exhausted | panic and shout
check: tf | Sunscreen should be put on again after swimming. | true
check: mcq | Where's the safest place to swim at a patrolled beach? | Between the red and yellow flags* | Next to the rocks | Wherever is empty
home: Before your next beach trip, check the conditions and patrol times with a grown-up.
skill: sun-water-safety

@lesson 1.4 | First aid basics | 🩹 | home
hook: Someone gets hurt. What are the first things you do?
learn: First, check for danger to yourself and others. Then get help: in an emergency call {nz:111~au:000}, or get a grown-up.
learn: For a minor burn, cool it under cool running water for 20 minutes. Don't use ice, butter or creams.
learn: For a bleeding cut, press firmly on it with a clean cloth.
try: Danger first, then help :: mcq | What's the emergency phone number? | {nz:111~au:000}* | 123 | 555
play: order | What to do when someone's hurt | Check for danger; Call for help; Help the person if it's safe; Stay with them until help arrives
check: mcq | How long should you cool a burn under running water? | 20 minutes* | 10 seconds | Don't: put butter on it
check: tf | You should press on a bleeding cut with a clean cloth. | true
check: mcq | What's the first thing to check when someone is hurt? | Whether there's danger* | Their phone | What they had for lunch
home: With a grown-up, find your home's first aid kit and check what's in it.
skill: first-aid
