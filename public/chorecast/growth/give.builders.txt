@lesson 1.4 | Check before you give | 🔍 | online
hook: A website asks for donations for a "very sad cause" with no details. Hmm.
learn: Scammers sometimes pretend to be charities, especially after disasters.
learn: Check the charity is registered and give through its official website.
play: scam | Real charity request or scam? | A registered charity's website with its charity number=real; A message from a stranger asking for gift cards for flood victims=scam; Your school's notice about a food bank drive=real; Pay now or the puppies will die! Bank transfer only=scam | Gift cards, pressure and no details are warning signs.
check: tf | Real charities ask for donations in gift cards. | false
check: mcq | How can you check a charity? | Look it up on the charity register* | Trust any message | Ask a stranger
check: mcq | When are charity scams common? | After big disasters* | Never | Only at Christmas
home: Find the registration number on a charity's website.
skill: scams
