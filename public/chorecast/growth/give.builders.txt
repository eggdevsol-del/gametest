@lesson 1.1 | Where does the money go? | 🏥 | townhall
hook: You give $10 to a charity. Where does it actually go?
learn: Charities spend money on their programmes, staff, and fundraising.
learn: Staff and running costs aren't bad. Good charities need skilled people.
learn: You can look up registered charities: {nz:charities.govt.nz~au:acnc.gov.au}.
play: match | Match each charity cost to what it pays for | Programmes=Food parcels for families; Staff=A nurse at a health clinic; Fundraising=Posters for an appeal; Running costs=Rent for the office
check: mcq | Where can you check if a charity is registered? | {nz:charities.govt.nz~au:acnc.gov.au}* | A random website | Nowhere
check: tf | All charity spending on staff is wasted. | false | Skilled staff help charities do good work.
check: mcq | What are programmes? | The actual helping work* | The logo | The CEO's car
home: Look up a charity you know on {nz:charities.govt.nz~au:acnc.gov.au}.
skill: charities

@lesson 1.3 | Plan a fundraiser | 🧁 | stall
hook: Your class wants to raise $200 for the local shelter. Where to start?
learn: Set a goal, pick an idea, plan costs, then tell people.
learn: Remember: costs come out first. You give the profit.
play: order | Put the fundraiser steps in order | Choose your cause and goal; Pick an idea, like a bake sale; Work out costs and price; Tell people about it; Run it and count the money; Give the money and say thanks
check: mcq | What should you do before the event? | Plan costs and prices* | Spend all the money | Nothing
check: gap | You raised $260 and costs were $60. You can give $_ | 200
check: tf | Thanking people who helped is part of fundraising. | true
home: Plan a mini fundraiser idea on paper with a goal.
skill: fundraising

@lesson 1.4 | Check before you give | 🔍 | online
hook: A website asks for donations for a "very sad cause" with no details. Hmm.
learn: Scammers sometimes pretend to be charities, especially after disasters.
learn: Check the charity is registered and give through its official website.
play: scam | Real charity request or scam? | A registered charity's website with its charity number=real; A message from a stranger asking for gift cards for flood victims=scam; Your school's notice about a food bank drive=real; Pay now or the puppies will die! Bank transfer only=scam | Gift cards, pressure and no details are warning signs.
check: tf | Real charities ask for donations in gift cards. | false
check: mcq | How can you check a charity? | Look it up on the charity register* | Trust any message | Ask a stranger
check: mcq | When are charity scams common? | After big disasters* | Never | Only at Christmas
home: Find the registration number on a charity's website.
skill: scams

@lesson 1.6 | Muhammad Yunus | 🏦 | museum
hook: Could lending someone $27 change their life?
learn: Muhammad Yunus, an economist in Bangladesh, lent small amounts to women in a village who couldn't get bank loans.
learn: They used the loans to start small businesses and paid them back.
learn: He started Grameen Bank and won the Nobel Peace Prize in 2006.
play: mystery | He's an economist from Bangladesh; He lent tiny amounts to people banks ignored; He started Grameen Bank; He won the Nobel Peace Prize in 2006 | Muhammad Yunus* | Warren Buffett | Andrew Carnegie
check: mcq | What did Yunus give people? | Small loans to start businesses* | Free houses | Cars
check: tf | Small loans can help people start businesses. | true
check: mcq | What prize did he win? | The Nobel Peace Prize* | An Olympic medal | A music award
home: Think of a small business someone could start with $30.
skill: impact
