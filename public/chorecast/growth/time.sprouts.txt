@lesson 1.1 | Swapping long ago | 🐑 | museum
hook: Before shops, how did people get things?
learn: Long ago, people swapped things they had for things they needed.
learn: A farmer might swap wool for fish.
play: match | Who might swap with whom? | Farmer with wool=Fisher with fish; Baker with bread=Potter with bowls; Hunter with skins=Gardener with vegetables
check: mcq | What is swapping things called? | Bartering* | Banking | Borrowing
check: tf | People have always used coins. | false
check: mcq | What was hard about swapping? | The other person had to want your thing* | It was too easy | Nothing
home: Find something in your house that people long ago might have swapped.
skill: barter

@lesson 1.2 | Shells as money | 🐚 | museum
hook: Could a shell be money?
learn: In many places, people used special shells, stones or beads as money.
learn: They were hard to find, easy to carry and hard to fake.
play: tap | Tap things people once used as money | 🐚:yes:Cowrie shells were used in Africa and Asia; 🪨:yes:Large stones were used on the island of Yap; 🧂:yes:Salt was valuable and traded; 🍕:no; 📱:no; 🪙:yes:Coins came later
check: mcq | Why were special shells good money? | Hard to find and easy to carry* | They were tasty | They were huge
check: tf | Salt was once valuable enough to trade. | true
check: mcq | Which came later? | Coins* | Swapping | Shells
home: Look for a shell or stone and imagine using it as money.
skill: history

@lesson 1.3 | The first coins | 🪙 | museum
hook: The first coins were made about 2,600 years ago. Where?

learn: Coins were stamped so people knew what they were worth.
play: order | Put money history in order | Swapping things; Using shells and beads; Making metal coins; Printing paper notes; Paying with cards and phones
check: mcq | Where were some of the first coins made? | Lydia, now part of Türkiye* | New Zealand | The moon
check: tf | Coins were stamped to show their value. | true
check: mcq | What came after coins? | Paper notes* | Shells | Swapping
home: Look closely at a coin. What pictures are stamped on it?
skill: history
