@lesson 1.1 | Fiat money | 💵 | bank
hook: Your $20 note isn't backed by gold. So why is it worth anything?
learn: Modern money is "fiat" money. It's valuable because people trust it and the law says it must be accepted for debts.
learn: Most money is created when banks make loans, as deposits in accounts.
play: debate | What gives modern money its value? | Trust, law and a stable economy* | Gold locked in a vault | Some people argue gold-backed money would be more stable. Most economists think fiat money gives more flexibility in a crisis.
check: mcq | What is fiat money? | Money valued by trust and law, not gold* | Money made of gold | Bartering
check: tf | Most money today exists as bank deposits. | true
check: mcq | How is most new money created? | When banks make loans* | Printing only | Mining
home: Read about how money is created on {nz:the Reserve Bank's~au:the RBA's} education pages.
skill: money

@lesson 1.2 | Enron | 📊 | townhall
hook: A giant energy company looked super profitable. Then it collapsed in weeks. How?
learn: Enron hid debts and invented profits with accounting tricks.
learn: When the truth came out in 2001, the company went bankrupt and its auditor collapsed too.
learn: New laws made company leaders and auditors more responsible for honest accounts.
play: diff | What Enron reported and what was true. Tap the differences. | Profits: growing fast; Debts: small; Auditor: independent; Shares: a safe bet | Profits: partly invented; Debts: hidden off the books; Auditor: compromised; Shares: almost worthless
check: mcq | What did Enron hide? | Debts and real losses* | Its logo | Its office
check: tf | Auditors are meant to check accounts independently. | true
check: mcq | When did Enron collapse? | 2001* | 1929 | 2020
home: Find out what an auditor does.
skill: governance

@lesson 1.3 | FTX | 🪙 | online
hook: A crypto exchange worth billions collapsed in days in 2022. Where did customers' money go?
learn: FTX was a crypto exchange. Customers deposited money to trade.
learn: Customer funds were secretly used by a related trading firm. When people rushed to withdraw, the money wasn't there.
learn: Its founder, Sam Bankman-Fried, was convicted of fraud in 2023.
play: scam | Warning sign or normal? | An exchange mixes customer money with its own trading=scam; Customer money kept separate and audited=real; Promises of guaranteed high returns=scam; Clear fees and regular independent audits=real | Mixing customer funds and guaranteed returns are red flags.
check: mcq | What went wrong at FTX? | Customer money was used without permission* | A website crash only | Nothing
check: tf | Customer funds should be kept separate from a company's own money. | true
check: mcq | What happened to the founder? | Convicted of fraud in 2023* | Became prime minister | Nothing
home: Read one article about why keeping customer funds separate matters.
skill: scams

@lesson 1.4 | Exchange rates | 💱 | bank
hook: Your trip to Japan suddenly costs more. Why?
learn: An exchange rate is how much of one currency buys another.
learn: Rates move with interest rates, trade, and how confident investors feel.
try: If $1 buys ¥90, then $100 buys ¥9,000 :: gap | If $1 buys ¥90, ¥4,500 costs $_ | 50

check: mcq | If your dollar buys fewer yen, overseas trips cost… | More* | Less | The same
check: tf | Exchange rates change over time. | true
check: mcq | What can move exchange rates? | Interest rates and trade* | The weather only | Nothing
home: Look up today's exchange rate to the yen or euro.
skill: currency
