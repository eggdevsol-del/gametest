@lesson 1.1 | Money seeds | 🌱 | shares
hook: Can a coin grow like a seed?
learn: Spending money uses it up. It's gone.
learn: Saving money keeps it. It stays the same size.
learn: Growing money means putting it somewhere that adds more over time.
play: sort | Grows, Stays the same, Gets used up | Money in a savings account=Grows; Coins under your bed=Stays the same; Buying lollies=Gets used up; Shares in a company=Grows; Money in your piggy bank=Stays the same; A movie ticket=Gets used up | Where does each money go?
check: mcq | Which one can make money grow? | A savings account* | Spending it | Hiding it under the bed
check: tf | Money under your bed gets bigger by itself. | false
check: mcq | What happens to money you spend? | It's used up* | It grows | It comes back
home: Ask a grown-up where their money grows.
skill: growth

@lesson 1.2 | The money tree | 🌳 | shares
hook: If you planted a money tree today, when would it be biggest?
learn: Growing money takes time, like a tree.
learn: Each year it adds a little more. Then that grows too!
play: order | Grow your money tree | Plant your savings; Wait a year; A little extra grows; The extra grows too; Your tree is much bigger
check: mcq | What does growing money need most? | Time* | Luck | Shouting at it
check: tf | A money tree grows faster when you dig it up every day. | false | Taking money out stops it growing.
check: mcq | When is a money tree biggest? | After many years* | On the first day | Never
home: Draw your money tree and how big it might be in 10 years.
skill: growth

@lesson 1.3 | Waiting pays | ⏳ | garden
hook: One treat now, or two treats if you wait? What would you choose?
learn: Waiting for something better is called patience.
learn: People who save and wait can often get bigger things.
play: path | You can have one cookie now, or two cookies if you wait 10 minutes. | Wait for two => good: You waited and got two! Waiting is a superpower. | Eat one now => ok: Yum! But you missed out on the second cookie. | Grab the cookie jar => bad: That's not the deal. Waiting is part of the game.
check: mcq | What is patience? | Waiting for something better* | Getting everything now | Giving up
check: tf | Saving and waiting can help you buy bigger things. | true
check: mcq | You want a $20 toy. You get $5 a week. What helps? | Save for 4 weeks* | Spend it each week | Cry
home: Pick something you'd like and work out how many weeks to save.
skill: patience
