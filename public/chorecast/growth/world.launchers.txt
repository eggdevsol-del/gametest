hook: You want your city to build safer bike lanes. Who actually has the power to make it happen?

@lesson 8.4 | Rights at work | 👷 | workshop
hook: Your boss says you have to come in an hour early for "training", unpaid. Is that OK?
learn: Workers have rights, like at least the minimum wage, breaks, safe workplaces and paid leave.
learn: Time your boss requires you to work, including training, usually has to be paid.
learn: You have the right to join a union. Unions bargain together for better pay and conditions, and your employer can't punish you for joining.
