@lesson 1.1 | Why rules? | 📏 | townhall
hook: What would a game be like with no rules?
learn: Rules help everyone be safe and treated fairly.
learn: Families, schools and countries all have rules.
play: sort | Fair rule, Unfair rule | Everyone takes turns on the slide=Fair rule; Only the biggest kid plays=Unfair rule; Hands up to talk in class=Fair rule; One person gets all the cake=Unfair rule; Everyone helps tidy up=Fair rule | Fair or unfair?
check: mcq | Why do we have rules? | To keep things safe and fair* | To be boring | No reason
check: tf | Countries have rules called laws. | true
check: mcq | A fair rule is one that… | Treats everyone fairly* | Helps only one person | Changes every minute
home: Make a fair rule with your family for a game.
skill: fairness

@lesson 1.4 | Voting | 🗳️ | townhall
hook: Your class can't agree: pizza day or movie day. How do you decide fairly?
learn: Voting lets everyone have a say. The choice with the most votes wins.
learn: Grown-ups vote to choose leaders.
play: order | Run a fair class vote | List the choices; Everyone gets one vote; Count the votes; The choice with the most votes wins; Everyone accepts the result
check: mcq | How many votes does each person get? | One* | As many as they want | None
check: tf | Voting gives everyone a say. | true
check: mcq | Who wins a vote? | The choice with the most votes* | The loudest person | The tallest person
home: Hold a family vote for dinner or a movie.
skill: voting
