@lesson 2.2 | When to call | ☎️ | townhall
hook: Is a lost toy an emergency? What about smoke pouring out of a house?
learn: An emergency is when someone is in danger or badly hurt, or there's a fire or a crime happening now.
learn: A lost toy or a scraped knee isn't an emergency. Tell a grown-up instead.
learn: If you call, stay calm, and say what's happening and where you are.
play: sort | Call {nz:111~au:000}, Tell a grown-up | Smoke pouring out of a house=Call {nz:111~au:000}; A lost toy=Tell a grown-up; Someone won't wake up=Call {nz:111~au:000}; A small scrape=Tell a grown-up; A car crash with people hurt=Call {nz:111~au:000}; A hole in your sock=Tell a grown-up | Emergency or not?
check: mcq | Which is an emergency? | Someone won't wake up* | A lost pencil | A boring day
check: tf | You should call {nz:111~au:000} about a lost toy. | false | Tell a grown-up instead. Emergency lines are for danger.
check: mcq | When you call for help, what should you say? | What's happening and where you are* | Your favourite colour | Nothing
home: Talk with a grown-up about what counts as an emergency.
skill: safety
