@lesson 2.4 | The deflation spiral | 🌀 | museum
hook: Prices falling sounds great. Why was it a disaster in the 1930s?
learn: In the Depression, prices fell year after year. That's called deflation.
learn: If prices will be lower next month, people wait to buy. Businesses sell less, so they cut jobs and wages.
learn: Then people have even less to spend, and prices fall again. It's a spiral.
learn: Debts feel heavier too, because the dollars you owe are worth more.
play: build | Build the deflation spiral | Prices start falling; People wait to buy; Businesses sell less; Businesses cut jobs and wages; People have less to spend; Prices fall even further
check: mcq | What is deflation? | Prices falling over time* | Prices rising over time | Letting air out of a tyre
check: tf | Deflation can make debts harder to repay. | true
check: mcq | Why do people put off buying during deflation? | They expect lower prices later* | Shops are closed | Money disappears
home: Explain the deflation spiral to someone at home in your own words.
