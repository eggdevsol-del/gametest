@lesson 2.3 | Stains | 🍅 | home

@lesson 3.3 | Nosebleeds | 👃 | home
hook: Your friend's nose starts bleeding. Head back or head forward?

learn: Pinch the soft part of the nose, below the bony bit, for about 10 minutes.

@lesson 3.4 | Sprains | 🦶 | home
hook: You roll your ankle at netball and it swells up. What helps?
learn: Remember RICE: Rest, Ice, Compression and Elevation.
learn: Rest it, wrap a cold pack in a cloth and hold it on for about 20 minutes, use a firm bandage, and raise it up.
learn: See a doctor if you can't walk on it or it looks out of shape.

check: mcq | When should you see a doctor about a sprain? | If you can't walk on it or it looks out of shape* | Never | Only after a month

@lesson 3.6 | The recovery position | 🛌 | home
hook: Someone has fainted but is breathing. What position keeps them safe while help comes?
learn: The recovery position is lying on their side, so their airway stays clear.
learn: Their top leg is bent to stop them rolling, and their head tilted back slightly.
learn: Stay with them and keep checking their breathing until help arrives.
play: build | Put someone in the recovery position | Kneel beside them; Put the arm nearest you at a right angle; Bring the other arm across their chest; Bend their far knee up; Roll them towards you onto their side; Tilt their head back slightly to keep the airway open

check: mcq | When is the recovery position used? | When someone is unconscious but breathing* | When someone is sprinting | When someone is eating
