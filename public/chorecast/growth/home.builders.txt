@lesson 1.3 | First aid for cuts | 🩹 | home
hook: A friend cuts their finger. What do you do first?
learn: Wash your hands, press a clean cloth on the cut to stop bleeding, then rinse and cover it.
learn: Get a grown-up for deep cuts or if bleeding won't stop. {nz:Call 111~au:Call 000} in an emergency.
play: order | Put first aid for a small cut in order | Wash your hands; Press a clean cloth on the cut; Rinse it with clean water; Cover it with a plaster; Tell a grown-up

check: tf | Deep cuts need a grown-up's help. | true
check: mcq | For a minor burn, you should… | Cool it under cool running water for 20 minutes* | Put butter on it | Put ice straight on it
home: Find where your family keeps the first aid kit.
skill: firstaid

@lesson 2.1 | Care labels | 🏷️ | home
hook: Inside your T-shirt is a tiny label covered in odd symbols. What do they mean?
learn: Care labels tell you how to wash, dry and iron clothes.
learn: A tub means washing. Dots show how warm. A hand in the tub means hand wash only.
learn: A square is for drying, an iron is for ironing, and a cross through any symbol means don't.

check: tf | A tub with a hand in it means hand wash only. | true
check: mcq | Where do you find care instructions? | On the label inside the clothes* | On the washing machine lid | In the fridge

@lesson 2.2 | Load the washer | 🌀 | home
hook: You stuff the whole basket into the machine and pour in heaps of powder. What could go wrong?
learn: Empty every pocket first. Tissues, coins and pens can wreck a wash.
learn: Don't overfill. Clothes need room to move to get clean.
learn: Use the amount of detergent on the pack. More isn't cleaner, just harder to rinse out.
play: build | Load the washing machine | Empty all the pockets; Do up the zips; Put clothes in loosely; Add detergent as the pack says; Pick the right setting; Press start
check: mcq | What can a tissue left in a pocket do? | Leave fluff all over the wash* | Make clothes smell nice | Nothing
check: tf | More detergent always means cleaner clothes. | false | Use the amount on the pack.
check: mcq | Why not overfill the machine? | Clothes need room to move* | It gets lonely | It washes faster
home: Load the washing machine with a grown-up watching.
skill: laundry

@lesson 2.3 | Stains | 🍅 | home
hook: Tomato sauce on your white shirt! Quick, what do you do?
learn: Act fast. Fresh stains come out much more easily.
learn: Blot with a cloth. Don't rub, or the stain spreads.
learn: Use cold water for blood and most food stains. Hot water can set some stains.
play: path | You drip tomato sauce on your shirt. | Blot it, rinse with cold water and treat it before washing => good: Most of it lifts out. | Rub it hard with a hot cloth => bad: Rubbing spreads it, and heat can set the stain. | Leave it in the basket for a week => bad: Old stains are much harder to shift.
check: mcq | What water is best for a blood stain? | Cold* | Boiling | No water at all
check: tf | You should rub a stain hard. | false | Blot it so it doesn't spread.
check: mcq | When is a stain easiest to remove? | Straight away* | After a month | Never
home: Help a grown-up treat a stain this week.
