@lesson 1.1 | Fixed and changing costs | 📌 | home
hook: Rent is the same each week. Groceries never are. Why does that matter?
learn: Fixed costs stay the same, like rent, a phone plan or insurance.
learn: Changing costs move around, like food, power, fuel and fun.
learn: Cover fixed costs first, then plan the changing ones with room to spare.

@lesson 2.3 | Automatic payments and direct debits | 🔁 | bank
hook: Your gym still takes $20 a week, three months after you stopped going. Why?
learn: An automatic payment is one you set up to send money on a schedule. You control it.
learn: A direct debit lets a company take money from your account. Cancel with the company and check your bank.
learn: Make sure there's enough money on the day, or you could get a dishonour fee.

@lesson 2.4 | Banking scams | 🚨 | online

learn: Your bank will never ask you to move money to a "safe account" or share a login code.
learn: Scammers fake bank phone numbers and texts. Hang up and call the number on your card.
learn: If you've sent money to a scammer, call your bank straight away. Speed matters.
