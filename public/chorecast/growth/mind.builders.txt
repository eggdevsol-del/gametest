@lesson 1.1 | Tiny habits | 🌱 | garden
hook: Reading one page a day doesn't sound like much. How many pages in a year?
learn: Tiny habits add up. One page a day is 365 pages a year.
learn: Link a new habit to something you already do: "After I brush my teeth, I read one page."
try: 5 minutes a day for a week is 35 minutes :: gap | 10 minutes a day for 30 days = _ minutes | 300
play: match | Link each new habit to an old one | After breakfast=Put my plate in the dishwasher; After brushing my teeth=Read one page; After school=Unpack my bag; Before bed=Lay out tomorrow's clothes
check: mcq | What makes a new habit easier? | Linking it to something you already do* | Doing it once | Waiting for motivation
check: tf | Small habits can add up to big results. | true
check: gap | 1 page a day for a year = _ pages | 365
home: Pick one tiny habit and link it to something you already do.
skill: habits

@lesson 1.2 | Wait for it | 🍪 | garden
hook: $5 now, or $8 if you wait a month?
learn: Delayed gratification means waiting for something better.
learn: It's a skill that helps with saving, study and goals.
play: path | You've saved $40 toward an $80 bike. A $35 game goes on sale. | Keep saving for the bike => good: Two more months and the bike is yours, and you'll use it every day. | Buy the game => ok: Fun now, but the bike is a long way off again. | Borrow money to get both => bad: Now you owe money and still need to save.
check: mcq | What is delayed gratification? | Waiting for something better* | Getting everything now | Giving up
check: tf | Delayed gratification helps with saving. | true
check: mcq | A trick for waiting? | Keep the goal where you can see it* | Look at ads all day | Think about the treat constantly
home: Put a picture of your saving goal where you'll see it every day.
skill: patience

@lesson 1.3 | Bouncing back | 🏀 | garden
hook: You missed the winning shot. Everyone saw. Now what?
learn: Resilience means bouncing back after hard times.
learn: Talk to someone, take care of yourself, and think about what you'd try next time.
play: sort | Helps you bounce back, Keeps you stuck | Talking to a friend or grown-up=Helps you bounce back; Saying "I'm useless"=Keeps you stuck; Practising the shot=Helps you bounce back; Quitting the team that day=Keeps you stuck; Getting some sleep=Helps you bounce back | Which helps?
check: mcq | What is resilience? | Bouncing back after hard times* | Never feeling sad | Giving up
check: tf | Talking to someone can help you bounce back. | true
check: mcq | After a mistake, a helpful thought is… | What could I try next time?* | I'm useless | Everyone hates me
home: Think of a time you bounced back. What helped?
skill: resilience

@lesson 1.4 | Screen balance | 📱 | home
hook: Why do apps make it so hard to stop scrolling?
learn: Many apps are designed to keep you watching with autoplay and endless feeds.
learn: Setting limits and taking breaks helps you stay in charge.
play: sort | Puts you in charge, Keeps you hooked | Turning off autoplay=Puts you in charge; Endless scrolling feeds=Keeps you hooked; Setting a timer=Puts you in charge; Notifications all night=Keeps you hooked; Phone out of the bedroom=Puts you in charge | Which is which?
check: mcq | Why is it hard to stop scrolling? | Apps are designed to keep you watching* | Your thumb is stuck | No reason
check: tf | Turning off autoplay helps you stop. | true
check: mcq | A good screen habit? | Phones out of bedrooms at night* | Scrolling until 2am | Notifications always on
home: Set a screen time goal with a grown-up for one week.
skill: screens

@lesson 1.5 | Worry tools | 🧰 | garden
hook: Your mind keeps saying "what if…?" How do you quiet it?
learn: Worry is normal. Name it, breathe, and talk about it.
learn: Ask: is this likely? What can I do about it?
play: order | Use the worry tools in order | Notice the worry; Name it out loud or write it down; Take five slow breaths; Ask: what's one thing I can do?; Talk to someone you trust
check: mcq | A helpful worry question? | What's one thing I can do?* | What if everything goes wrong? | Why me?
check: tf | Worrying sometimes is normal. | true
check: mcq | Who can you talk to about worries? | A trusted grown-up* | Nobody | A stranger online
home: Write down one worry and one thing you can do about it.
skill: worry

@lesson 1.6 | "Not yet" | 🌟 | garden
hook: "I can't do fractions." What if you added one word: "yet"?
learn: Brains grow with practice and effort.
learn: "I can't do it yet" reminds you that skills grow.
play: match | Change each fixed thought to a growth thought | I'm bad at maths=I'm getting better with practice; I give up=I'll try a different way; This is too hard=This might take time and effort; I made a mistake=Mistakes help me learn
check: mcq | Which is a growth thought? | I can't do it yet* | I'll never do it | I'm just dumb
check: tf | Practice helps your brain grow. | true
check: mcq | What helps skills grow? | Practice and effort* | Luck only | Waiting
home: Catch yourself saying "I can't" and add "yet".
skill: growth
