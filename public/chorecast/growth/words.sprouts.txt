@lesson 1.1 | Magic words | ✨ | stage
hook: Which words make people smile and want to help?
learn: "Please" asks nicely. "Thank you" shows you're grateful.
learn: "Sorry" fixes things when you've made a mistake.
play: match | Match the moment to the magic word | Asking for the salt=Please; Getting a present=Thank you; Bumping into someone=Sorry; Someone holds the door=Thank you
check: mcq | You want a turn on the swing. You say… | Can I have a turn please?* | Get off! | Nothing, just push
check: tf | Saying thank you shows you're grateful. | true
check: mcq | You knock over a friend's tower. You say… | Sorry, I'll help fix it* | Ha ha | It was already wobbly
home: Count how many times you say please and thank you today.
skill: manners

@lesson 1.2 | Asking for help | 🙋 | stage
hook: You're stuck on a puzzle. What do you say?
learn: Good questions say what you need. "Can you help me find the edge pieces?"
learn: Asking for help is brave, not silly.
play: mcq | Which question is clearest? | Can you show me how to tie this knot?* | Help! | Do the thing
check: tf | Asking for help is a brave thing to do. | true
check: mcq | Who can you ask for help at school? | A teacher* | Nobody | A stranger outside
check: mcq | A good question says… | What you need* | Nothing | Something rude
home: Ask a grown-up for help with something, using a clear question.
skill: asking

@lesson 1.3 | Good listening | 👂 | stage
hook: How can people tell you're really listening?
learn: Good listeners look at the speaker, stay quiet, and ask a question after.
play: tap | Tap the good listening habits | 👀:yes:Looking at the person; 🤐:yes:Waiting for your turn; 📱:no; 🙋:yes:Asking a question after; 🙉:no; 👍:yes:Nodding to show you understand
check: mcq | Which is good listening? | Looking at the speaker* | Playing on a phone | Talking over them
check: tf | Interrupting shows good listening. | false
check: mcq | After someone talks, a good listener might… | Ask a question about it* | Walk away | Yawn loudly
home: Listen to someone's story and ask them one question about it.
skill: listening

@lesson 1.4 | Story order | 📖 | stage
hook: A story about a lost puppy starts with "And they lived happily ever after". Weird?
learn: Stories have a beginning, a middle and an end.
learn: Words like first, then, next and finally help people follow along.
play: order | Put the story in order | First, Ruby's puppy ran away; Then she looked in the park; Next she asked her neighbours; Finally she found him under the slide
check: mcq | Which word starts a story? | First* | Finally | The end
check: tf | Stories have a beginning, middle and end. | true
check: mcq | Which word comes last? | Finally* | First | Then
home: Tell a grown-up about your day using first, then, finally.
skill: storytelling

@lesson 1.5 | A thank-you card | 💌 | stage
hook: Your aunty sent you a gift. How do you say thanks so she knows you mean it?
learn: A good thank-you says what you got, why you like it, and who it's from.
play: order | Put the thank-you card in order | Dear Aunty Mere; Thank you for the art set; I've already drawn our cat with it; Love from Ava
check: mcq | What should a thank-you card say? | What you got and why you like it* | Nothing | What you wanted instead
check: tf | Saying why you like a gift makes a thank-you special. | true
check: mcq | How do you start a card? | Dear… * | Hey you | The end
home: Write a thank-you card to someone this week.
skill: writing

@lesson 1.6 | Feeling words | 😊 | garden
hook: How do you tell someone how you feel without shouting?
learn: Feeling words help people understand you: happy, sad, worried, cross, excited.
learn: Try "I feel… because…".
play: match | Match the feeling to the face | Happy=😄; Sad=😢; Worried=😟; Cross=😠; Excited=🤩
check: mcq | Which sentence uses a feeling word well? | I feel sad because my toy broke* | Waaah! | I'm going to throw this
check: tf | Using feeling words helps people understand you. | true
check: mcq | 😟 means… | Worried* | Happy | Sleepy
home: Use "I feel… because…" once today.
skill: feelings
