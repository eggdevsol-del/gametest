@lesson 1.1 | Magic words | ✨ | stage
hook: Which words make people smile and want to help?
learn: "Please" asks nicely. "Thank you" shows you're grateful.
learn: "Sorry" fixes things when you've made a mistake.
play: match | Match the moment to the magic word | Asking for the salt=Please; Getting a present=Thank you; Bumping into someone=Sorry; Someone holds the door=Thank you
check: mcq | You want a turn on the swing. You say… | Can I have a turn please?* | Get off! | Nothing, just push
check: tf | Saying thank you shows you're grateful. | true
check: mcq | You knock over a friend's tower. You say… | Sorry, I'll help fix it* | Ha ha | It was already wobbly
home: Count how many times you say please and thank you today.

@lesson 3.6 | Wait your turn | ⏳ | stage
hook: You've got something exciting to say, but your friend is still talking. What now?
learn: Interrupting means talking while someone else is still talking.
learn: Hold your idea in your head and wait for them to finish.
learn: If it's urgent, say "Excuse me" first.
play: path | Your friend is telling a story. You suddenly remember your big news. | Wait until they finish, then share your news => good: Your friend felt heard, and then they listened to you too. | Shout your news over the top of them => bad: Your friend felt like you didn't care about their story. | Walk away to tell someone else => bad: Your friend was left talking to nobody.
check: mcq | What does interrupting mean? | Talking while someone else is talking* | Listening quietly | Taking turns
check: tf | If it's urgent, you can say "Excuse me" first. | true
check: mcq | You have news but someone is talking. You… | Wait for them to finish* | Shout it out | Cover their mouth
home: Play a talking game with a teddy as the "talking turn". Only the person holding it talks.
