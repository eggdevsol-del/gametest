@lesson 2.3 | The minimum payment trap | 🪤 | bank

learn: Pay as much as you can. The full balance is best.

check: mcq | Why is paying only the minimum risky? | The debt shrinks slowly and costs lots of interest* | The bank closes your account | It's against the law
