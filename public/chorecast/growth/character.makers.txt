@lesson 1.1 | Honest when it's hard | 🧭 | home
hook: You find $20 on the floor at school. No one saw. What now?
learn: Honesty means telling the truth and not taking what isn't yours, even when no one would know.
learn: Small lies make the next lie easier, and trust is slow to rebuild.
learn: Being honest when it costs you is how people learn they can rely on you.
play: path | You find $20 near the school office. No one saw. | Hand it in at the office => good: It may be someone's lunch money, and now people know you can be trusted. | Keep it, finders keepers => bad: It isn't yours, and you'd know. | Ask around for who lost it => ok: Kind, but the office can find the owner more fairly.
check: mcq | What builds trust fastest? | Being honest when it costs you* | Telling people what they want to hear | Never admitting a mistake
check: tf | Small lies don't matter if no one finds out. | false | They make the next lie easier and wear down trust.
home: Talk with your family about a time when being honest was hard but worth it.
skill: honesty
