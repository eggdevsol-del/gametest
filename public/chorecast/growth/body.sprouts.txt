# body · sprouts
@unit 1 | Healthy every day | Move, sleep, eat well and wash

@lesson 1.1 | Move your body | 🏃 | garden
hook: What happens to your heart when you run around?
learn: Moving your body makes your heart and muscles stronger.
learn: Kids should be active every day with games that make you puff, like tag, biking or dancing.
learn: Moving also helps you feel happy and sleep well.
try: Playing tag makes you puff :: mcq | Which is active play? | Riding a bike* | Watching TV | Lying on the couch
play: sort | Active, Not active | Jumping on a trampoline=Active; Playing video games=Not active; Swimming=Active; Reading on the couch=Not active; Dancing=Active; Kicking a ball=Active | Active or not?
check: tf | Moving every day helps your heart get stronger. | true
check: mcq | Moving your body can help you… | feel happy and sleep well* | get sick | feel tired forever
check: mcq | Which game makes you puff? | Tag* | Snap | Colouring in
home: Play an active game outside with your family for 20 minutes.
skill: being-active

@lesson 1.2 | Sleep | 😴 | home
hook: Why do you feel grumpy after a late night?
learn: Sleep lets your body grow and your brain sort out what you learned.
learn: Kids your age need lots of sleep: about 9 to 12 hours a night.
learn: A calm bedtime routine with no screens helps you fall asleep.
try: Going to bed on time helps :: mcq | How many hours of sleep do kids aged 7 to 9 need? | About 9 to 12* | About 4 | About 20
play: sort | Helps sleep, Gets in the way | Reading a book in bed=Helps sleep; Playing on a tablet in bed=Gets in the way; A dark, quiet room=Helps sleep; A fizzy drink at bedtime=Gets in the way; The same bedtime every night=Helps sleep | Does it help sleep?
check: tf | Your brain sorts out what you learned while you sleep. | true
check: mcq | Which helps you fall asleep? | A calm routine with no screens* | A scary movie | Jumping on the bed
check: mcq | After a late night, you might feel… | grumpy and tired* | full of energy | taller
home: Make a bedtime routine chart with a grown-up and try it all week.
skill: sleep

@lesson 1.3 | Water and food | 🍎 | kitchen
hook: Should you drink water or fizzy drink when you're thirsty?
learn: Water is the best drink for your body. Drink it through the day, especially when you're active or hot.
learn: Eating lots of different foods gives your body what it needs. Try fruit and vegetables, bread and grains, milk foods, and meat, fish, eggs or beans.
learn: Treats like lollies and fizzy drinks are sometimes foods, not everyday foods.
try: Water is the best drink :: mcq | What's the best drink when you're thirsty? | Water* | Fizzy drink | Cordial
play: sort | Everyday food, Sometimes food | Apple=Everyday food; Lollies=Sometimes food; Carrot sticks=Everyday food; Chips=Sometimes food; Wholemeal bread=Everyday food; Fizzy drink=Sometimes food | Everyday or sometimes?
check: mcq | When should you drink more water? | When you're active or hot* | Never | Only at dinner
check: tf | Eating lots of different foods is good for your body. | true
check: mcq | Which is an everyday food? | A banana* | A chocolate bar | A lolly
home: Help pack a lunch with something from each food group.
skill: food-water

@lesson 1.4 | Germs and washing hands | 🧼 | home
hook: Germs are too small to see. How do we stop them making us sick?
learn: Germs are tiny living things. Some of them can make us sick.
learn: Washing your hands with soap and water gets rid of germs. Rub for about 20 seconds, then rinse and dry.
learn: Wash before eating, after the toilet, and after touching animals.
try: Rub for about 20 seconds :: mcq | When should you wash your hands? | Before eating* | Only on birthdays | Never
play: order | Wash your hands in order | Wet your hands; Add soap; Rub for about 20 seconds; Rinse; Dry
check: tf | Washing with soap helps get rid of germs. | true
check: mcq | After feeding the hens, you should… | wash your hands* | lick your fingers | eat straight away
check: mcq | Cough or sneeze… | into your elbow* | onto your friend | into your hand, then shake hands
home: Hum "Happy Birthday" twice while you wash your hands. That's about 20 seconds.
skill: hygiene
