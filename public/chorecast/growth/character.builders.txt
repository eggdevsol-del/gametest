learn: Gratitude means noticing the good things you already have.

learn: Thank the people behind the good things, not just the things.

learn: If you still want it, you'll know it's worth it, and you've had time to save.

home: Start a 30-day want list with today's date.

@lesson 2.2 | Tricks for waiting | 🧠 | garden
hook: Your phone buzzes while you're doing homework. How do you not grab it?
learn: Put temptations out of reach. A phone in another room is easier to ignore.
learn: Make an if-then plan: "If I want my phone, then I'll finish one more question first."
learn: Think about the treat differently. Picture the treat as a toy or a cloud, not something yummy.
play: match | Match each problem to a waiting trick | Phone keeps buzzing=Put it in another room; Want to spend all your money=Leave your card at home; Lollies in the cupboard=Ask for them to go on a high shelf; Can't stop thinking about a game=Make an if-then plan
check: mcq | What's an if-then plan? | Deciding ahead what you'll do when tempted* | A maths question | A plan to give up
check: tf | Putting temptations out of reach makes waiting easier. | true
check: mcq | You want to save money at the mall. A good trick is… | Only take the money you plan to spend* | Take all your savings | Go every day
home: Write one if-then plan for something that tempts you.

@lesson 2.3 | Effort plus strategy | 🧩 | workshop
hook: You've tried the same maths question five times, the same way. Still wrong. Should you just try harder?
learn: Growth mindset isn't only about effort. Trying the same thing harder doesn't always work.
learn: Change your strategy: try a new method, break it into steps or look at an example.
learn: Asking for help is a strategy too. It's what good learners do.
play: path | You've got the same maths question wrong five times, the same way. | Try a different method, or ask for help => good: A new approach helps it click. That's a growth mindset in action. | Keep doing it exactly the same way, harder => bad: More effort on the same method usually gets the same result. | Give up and say "I'm bad at maths" => bad: You're not bad at maths. You just need a new strategy.
check: mcq | You're stuck after lots of tries. What helps? | Change your strategy or ask for help* | Keep doing it the same way | Give up
check: tf | Asking for help is a learning strategy. | true
check: mcq | Growth mindset is about… | Effort, new strategies and help* | Effort only | Being naturally smart
home: Next time you're stuck, try one new strategy before asking for help.
skill: growth

check: mcq | After an apology, it's important to… | actually do better* | do it again | expect a reward
