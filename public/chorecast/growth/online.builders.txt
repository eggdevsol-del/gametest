@lesson 1.1 | Spot the scam | 🎣 | online
hook: "You've won a free phone! Just pay $2 postage." Real?
learn: Scams try to get your money or information.
learn: Warning signs: prizes you didn't enter, urgency, secrecy, and requests for card details, codes or gift cards.
play: scam | Real or scam? | You won a phone! Pay $2 postage now=scam; Your school's newsletter with the term dates=real; Send us a gift card code to unlock your account=scam; Your friend asks to meet at the park after school (in person at school earlier)=real; Act now or lose your account forever!=scam | Prizes you didn't enter, urgency and gift cards are big red flags.
check: mcq | Which is a scam warning sign? | A prize for a competition you didn't enter* | Your teacher's email | A school notice
check: tf | Real companies ask for gift card codes. | false
check: mcq | You think something is a scam. You… | Don't reply, and tell a grown-up* | Pay just in case | Send your details
home: Show a grown-up one scam message and how you spotted it.
skill: scams

@lesson 1.4 | Two-step login | 📲 | online
hook: Someone guessed your password. Can they still get in?
learn: Two-factor authentication (2FA) needs your password plus a code from your phone or app.
learn: Even if someone has your password, they can't get in without the code.
play: order | Log in with 2FA in order | Type your username; Type your password; Get a code on your phone or app; Type the code; You're in
check: mcq | What does 2FA add? | A second code to log in* | A second password you share | Nothing
check: tf | You should share 2FA codes with people who message you. | false
check: mcq | Someone asks for your 2FA code. That's… | A scam* | Normal | Helpful
home: Turn on 2FA for one account with a grown-up.
skill: passwords

@lesson 1.6 | Standing up to cyberbullying | 🛑 | online
hook: Someone in your group chat keeps posting mean memes about a classmate. What do you do?
learn: Don't join in, don't share, and support the person being targeted.
learn: Save evidence and tell a grown-up. {nz:Netsafe (0508 638 723) can help.~au:The eSafety Commissioner can help.}
play: path | A group chat is mocking a classmate. | Don't share it, message the classmate kindly and tell a grown-up => good: That takes courage, and it helps the person feel less alone. | Leave the chat quietly => ok: You didn't join in, which is good. Telling a grown-up would help more. | Add a funny reply => bad: Joining in makes it worse and hurts someone.
check: mcq | You see cyberbullying. You… | Don't share, support them, tell a grown-up* | Share it | Join in
check: tf | Saving evidence can help stop bullying. | true
check: mcq | {nz:Who can help with online bullying in NZ?~au:Who can help with online bullying in Australia?} | {nz:Netsafe~au:The eSafety Commissioner}* | Nobody | The bully

@lesson 2.3 | Limited-time offers | ⏳ | online
hook: "Only 2 hours left! 50% off the mega bundle!" Do you buy it now?
learn: Countdown timers and "limited time" offers rush you so you don't stop and think.
learn: Battle passes and daily rewards make you feel you'll miss out if you stop playing or paying.
learn: Pause and ask: would I buy this if there was no timer?
play: path | A countdown says "2 hours left! 50% off!" | Wait and ask a grown-up, even if the deal ends => good: Another deal will come along. You stayed in control. | Buy it fast before the timer runs out => bad: The timer rushed you. You might not even want it tomorrow. | Use a saved card without asking => bad: That's spending money that isn't yours to spend.
check: mcq | Why do games use countdown timers? | To rush you into buying* | To help you relax | To tell the time
check: tf | Another "limited" deal usually comes along later. | true
check: mcq | A good question before buying a deal? | Would I buy this with no timer?* | How fast can I tap? | What's the biggest bundle?
home: Spot a countdown or "limited time" deal in a game or ad.

@lesson 2.4 | Purchase controls | 🔒 | online

learn: A card saved on a shared device should always be locked behind a password.

check: tf | A saved card on a shared device with no password is risky. | true
check: mcq | Who should know the purchase password? | A grown-up* | Everyone in your class | Your gaming friends
home: Check the purchase settings on a family device together.
