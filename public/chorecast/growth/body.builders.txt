@unit 1 | Fuel and move | Food, drinks, activity, teeth and sun

@lesson 1.1 | A balanced plate | 🍽️ | kitchen
hook: What does a healthy dinner look like?
learn: A balanced plate is about half vegetables and fruit, a quarter grains like rice, pasta or bread, and a quarter protein like meat, fish, eggs, tofu or beans.
learn: Milk, yoghurt and cheese help build strong bones.
learn: A mix of foods gives your body energy and helps it grow and repair.
try: Half the plate is vegetables and fruit :: mcq | About how much of a balanced plate should be vegetables and fruit? | Half* | None | All of it
play: sort | Vegetables and fruit, Grains, Protein | Broccoli=Vegetables and fruit; Rice=Grains; Chicken=Protein; Carrots=Vegetables and fruit; Pasta=Grains; Lentils=Protein | Which part of the plate?
check: mcq | Which food helps build strong bones? | Milk* | Lollies | Fizzy drink
check: tf | Eating a mix of foods helps your body grow and repair. | true
check: mcq | Which is a protein food? | Eggs* | Lettuce | Bread
home: Help plan one balanced dinner this week.
skill: balanced-plate

@lesson 1.2 | Drinks | 💧 | kitchen
hook: Sports drink, fizzy drink or water: which does your body need after sport?
learn: Water is the best everyday drink. Plain milk is good too.
learn: Fizzy drinks, energy drinks and many juices have lots of sugar, which is bad for your teeth.
learn: Energy drinks often have caffeine and aren't suitable for kids.
try: After sport, water is usually all you need :: mcq | What's the best everyday drink? | Water* | An energy drink | Fizzy drink
play: sort | Everyday drink, Sometimes or never | Water=Everyday drink; Energy drink=Sometimes or never; Plain milk=Everyday drink; Fizzy drink=Sometimes or never; Cordial=Sometimes or never | Which is it?
check: tf | Energy drinks are a good choice for kids. | false | They often have caffeine and lots of sugar. Water is better.
check: mcq | Sugary drinks are especially bad for your… | teeth* | hair | fingernails
check: mcq | After a game of football, most kids need… | water* | an energy drink | coffee
home: With a grown-up, compare the sugar on the labels of two drinks at home.
skill: healthy-drinks

@lesson 1.3 | An hour a day | 🚴 | garden
hook: How much moving should you do each day?
learn: Kids and teens should get at least an hour a day of moving that makes you breathe harder, like running, biking or swimming.
learn: A few times a week, do things that make your muscles and bones strong, like climbing, jumping or push-ups.
learn: It all counts: walking to school, carrying the washing, playing at lunch and sport.
try: Biking to school counts :: mcq | How much active time do kids need each day? | At least an hour* | 5 minutes | None
play: sort | Builds heart and lungs, Builds muscles and bones | Running=Builds heart and lungs; Climbing=Builds muscles and bones; Swimming=Builds heart and lungs; Push-ups=Builds muscles and bones; Biking=Builds heart and lungs; Hopping and jumping=Builds muscles and bones | What does it build most?
check: tf | Walking to school counts as active time. | true
check: mcq | Which builds strong bones? | Jumping and climbing* | Sitting still | Watching sport
check: mcq | Active time should make you… | breathe harder* | fall asleep | feel cold
home: Add up your active minutes today. Did you reach an hour?
skill: daily-activity

@lesson 1.4 | Teeth and skin | 🦷 | home
hook: Why do dentists say to brush twice a day?
learn: Brush your teeth twice a day for about two minutes with fluoride toothpaste. Spit out the extra toothpaste, but don't rinse.
learn: Sugary food and drinks feed germs that make holes in teeth.
learn: Protect your skin from the sun: {nz:slip, slop, slap and wrap~au:slip, slop, slap, seek and slide}.
try: Brush morning and night :: mcq | How long should you brush your teeth? | About two minutes* | Five seconds | Half an hour
play: match | Match each sun-safety word to what you do | Slip=Slip on a shirt; Slop=Slop on sunscreen; Slap=Slap on a hat
check: tf | Sugary drinks can cause holes in your teeth. | true
check: mcq | When should you brush your teeth? | Morning and night* | Once a week | Only before the dentist
check: mcq | Which helps protect your skin from the sun? | A hat and sunscreen* | Lying in the midday sun | Nothing
home: Check the sunscreen at home with a grown-up, and put a hat by the door.
skill: teeth-skin

@unit 2 | Safe and sound | Bikes, water, first aid and calling for help

@lesson 2.1 | Bike and scooter safety | 🚲 | townhall
hook: What's the most important thing to wear on a bike?
learn: Always wear a helmet that fits snugly and is done up.
learn: Be seen: bright clothes, lights at dusk, and a bell.
learn: Check your brakes and tyres before you ride, and watch out for car doors opening.
try: Helmet on and done up :: mcq | How should a helmet fit? | Snug and done up* | Loose on the back of your head | Unclipped
play: order | Before you ride | Put on your helmet and do it up; Check your brakes; Check your tyres; Look around before you set off
check: tf | Bright clothing helps drivers see you. | true
check: mcq | What's a danger to watch for when riding past parked cars? | Doors opening* | Birds | Puddles
check: mcq | Riding at dusk, you should use… | lights* | sunglasses | headphones
home: Do a bike check with a grown-up: helmet, brakes, tyres and lights.
skill: bike-safety

@lesson 2.2 | Safe in the water | 🌊 | island
hook: Why can even strong swimmers get into trouble?
learn: Conditions can change quickly. Waves, cold water and rips can tire anyone out.
learn: Swim between the flags, never swim alone, and check the conditions first.
learn: If you're caught in a rip, stay calm, float and raise your hand for help.
try: Float and raise your hand :: mcq | If you're caught in a rip, you should… | stay calm, float and raise your hand* | swim hard against it | panic
play: sort | Safe choice, Risky choice | Swimming between the flags=Safe choice; Swimming alone after dark=Risky choice; Wearing a life jacket in a kayak=Safe choice; Jumping off a wharf into water you can't see into=Risky choice; Asking a lifeguard about the conditions=Safe choice | Safe or risky?
check: tf | Cold water can tire you out quickly. | true
check: mcq | Before swimming at a new beach, you should… | check the conditions and the flags* | dive straight in | swim out as far as you can
check: mcq | A friend is in trouble in deep water. The safest first step is to… | call for help and throw them something that floats* | jump straight in | leave
home: With a grown-up, learn how to spot a rip: a calmer-looking, darker patch between breaking waves.
skill: water-safety-teen

@lesson 2.3 | First aid for small injuries | 🩹 | home
hook: Your little brother falls and cuts his knee. What do you do?
learn: For a small cut: wash your hands, rinse the cut with clean water, then cover it with a plaster.
learn: For a nosebleed: sit up, lean forward, and pinch the soft part of the nose for 10 minutes.
learn: For a bump: hold on a cold pack wrapped in a cloth for a short time. Tell a grown-up about any bump to the head.
try: Lean forward for a nosebleed :: mcq | For a nosebleed, you should… | lean forward and pinch the soft part of your nose* | lean back | lie down flat
play: order | Care for a small cut | Wash your hands; Rinse the cut with clean water; Pat it dry; Cover it with a plaster
check: tf | You should tell a grown-up about any bump to the head. | true
check: mcq | How long should you pinch a nosebleed? | About 10 minutes* | 5 seconds | An hour
check: mcq | A cold pack should be… | wrapped in a cloth* | put on bare skin for hours | heated first
home: With a grown-up, check what's in your home's first aid kit.
skill: first-aid-minor

@lesson 2.4 | Calling for help | 📞 | home
hook: What would you say if you called the emergency number?
learn: In an emergency, call {nz:111~au:000}. It works even from a phone with no credit.
learn: Stay calm. Say which service you need (ambulance, fire or police), where you are, and what happened.
learn: Don't hang up until they tell you to. They may give you instructions.
try: Service, address, what happened :: mcq | What should you tell the operator first? | Which service you need, and where you are* | Your favourite colour | Nothing
play: order | Make an emergency call | Call {nz:111~au:000}; Ask for the service you need; Give the address; Explain what happened; Stay on the line
check: tf | You should stay on the line until the operator says you can hang up. | true
check: mcq | Which is a reason to call the emergency number? | A house on fire* | A lost phone charger | Being bored
check: mcq | If you don't know the address, you can… | describe nearby landmarks* | hang up | make one up
home: Make sure you know your full address, including your town.
skill: emergency-call-teen
