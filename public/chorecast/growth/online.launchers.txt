@lesson 1.5 | Investment scams | 📉 | shares
hook: A friendly stranger online shows you their crypto profits and offers to help you invest. Nice?
learn: In "pig butchering" scams, scammers build trust over weeks, then push fake investment platforms.
learn: Fake platforms show big gains, but you can't withdraw. Then they ask for "fees".
play: scam | Real or scam? | A licensed fund you can check on the official register=real; A new online friend with a "secret" crypto platform=scam; Guaranteed 20% a month returns=scam; Your bank's investment app with clear fees=real; Pay a fee to withdraw your profits=scam | Guaranteed returns, secret platforms and withdrawal fees are huge red flags.

check: tf | You should check if an investment provider is licensed. | true
