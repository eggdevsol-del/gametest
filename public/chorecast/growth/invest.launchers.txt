@lesson 2.6 | Active or index? | 🧭 | shares
hook: One fund pays experts to pick shares. Another just buys the whole index. Which usually wins?
learn: Active funds try to beat the market by picking shares. They usually charge higher fees.
learn: Index funds buy everything in an index, for low fees.
learn: Studies keep finding that most active funds trail their index over 10 years or more, mainly because of fees.
play: path | You're picking a fund for the next 40 years. | A low-fee index fund that suits your timeline => good: Low fees and a broad spread. Check it keeps beating its index after fees, and watch those fees. | The fund with the biggest ad and the highest fees => bad: Ads don't grow your money, and high fees eat returns every year.
