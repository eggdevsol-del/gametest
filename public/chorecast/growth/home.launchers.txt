@lesson 1.4 | Insurance | ☂️ | home

learn: An excess is what you pay before insurance pays the rest.
learn: Cheaper premiums often mean higher excesses. Read what's covered.

@lesson 2.1 | Reading a power bill | 🔌 | home
hook: Your first power bill is $210. Is that a lot? Where does it come from?
learn: Most power bills have two parts: a daily fixed charge, and a price for each unit you use.
learn: A unit is a kilowatt-hour (kWh): 1,000 watts used for one hour.
learn: Bill = days × daily charge + kWh used × price per kWh, plus or minus any discounts.
try: 500 kWh × 30c = $150 :: gap | 400 kWh × 30c = $_ | 120
play: slider | 30 days at $1.50 a day, plus 450 kWh at 30c. What's the bill? | 100 | 300 | 180 | $ | 5 | 5
check: mcq | What's a kWh? | 1,000 watts used for one hour* | A kind of wire | A power company
check: gap | 30 days × a $2 daily charge = $_ | 60
check: tf | You pay the daily charge even if you use very little power. | true
home: Find the daily charge and the price per kWh on a real power bill.
