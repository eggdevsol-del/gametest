@lesson 1.4 | Insurance | ☂️ | home
hook: A burst pipe ruins your laptop and clothes. Who pays?
learn: Contents insurance covers your belongings if they're damaged or stolen.
learn: An excess is what you pay before insurance pays the rest.
learn: Cheaper premiums often mean higher excesses. Read what's covered.
try: $2,000 claim with $400 excess means insurance pays $1,600 :: gap | $1,500 claim, $300 excess. Insurance pays $_ | 1200
play: match | Match the word to its meaning | Premium=What you pay for the insurance; Excess=What you pay toward a claim; Claim=Asking the insurer to pay; Policy=The contract and what it covers
check: mcq | What is an excess? | The amount you pay before insurance pays* | A bonus | The premium
check: tf | Cheaper premiums often come with higher excesses. | true
check: mcq | What covers your belongings in a rental? | Contents insurance* | The landlord's insurance always | Nothing
home: Ask a grown-up what their insurance excess is.
