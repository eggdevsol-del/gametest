@lesson 1.1 | Hola | 👋 | home
hook: Spanish is spoken by more than 500 million people. Here’s the first word they all know.
word: hola | hello | 👋 | wave
say: «¡Hola, Ana!» | Hello, Ana!
say: «¡Hola, Leo!» | Hello, Leo!
learn: In Spanish, a shout or a question has a mark at the start too, upside down: «¡Hola!»
play: hear | Which one is «hola»? | 👋* | 🌙 | 🍎
check: hear | What does «hola» mean? | hello* | good night | thank you
check: mcq | Ana says «¡Hola, Leo!» What is she doing? | Saying hello to Leo* | Saying goodbye | Saying thank you
home: Say «hola» to everyone at home today.
skill: es-greet

@lesson 1.2 | Buenos días | 🌅 | home
hook: Spanish has a greeting for the morning, the afternoon and the night.
word: buenos días | good morning | 🌅 | stretch your arms up
word: buenas tardes | good afternoon | 🌤️ | shade your eyes from the sun
word: buenas noches | good evening, good night | 🌙 | rest your head on your hands
rule: «Buenos días» until lunch, «buenas tardes» in the afternoon, and «buenas noches» once it’s dark.
chant: «Buenos días»; «Buenas tardes»; «Buenas noches»
play: match | Match them | «buenos días»=good morning; «buenas tardes»=good afternoon; «buenas noches»=good night
check: mcq | It’s breakfast time. You say… | «¡Buenos días!»* | «¡Buenas noches!» | «¡Buenas tardes!»
check: hear | Which one is «buenas noches»? | 🌙* | 🌅 | 🌤️
home: Say «buenos días» at breakfast and «buenas noches» at bedtime.
skill: es-greet
